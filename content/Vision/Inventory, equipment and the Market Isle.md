### Inventory

The inventory has no weight, only slots. Carrying a lot never slows the character; the limit is how many items fit.

* The character starts with a base number of slots and buys expansions with in-game money. *Proposal: each expansion costs more than the last, up to a maximum.*  
* **The special tab** has unlimited space for character-bound items: quest items, character-bound currencies and points, and other special items. Quest items never take normal inventory space.  
* **Binding:** character-bound items stay only on the character, in the special tab, never in the bank or a house chest; account-bound items can be on the character and in the bank, not in a house chest; unbound items can go anywhere, house chests included. Quest items are always character-bound.  
* Trade packs don't take slots: they are world objects, carried on the back or in special vehicle slots, and they drop when the character dies or the vehicle is destroyed or dismissed. They don't let you fly or glide (see [[Movement and vehicles]]).

### Equipment slots

* **Combat (13):** main hand \+ off hand, or one 2H weapon; armor (head, chest, hands, legs, feet); jewelry (necklace, 2 rings, 2 earrings); cloak.  
* **Back:** glider or wing. A trade pack takes it over while carried. *Proposal: the pack overrides the slot without unequipping the glider, which comes back when the pack is dropped, and the cloak is hidden while something is on the back.*  
* **Profession (5):** head, hands, feet, body and tool, always active (see [[Real exploration#Professions|Professions]]).  
* There is no ranged slot, no instrument slot and no tool belt. The boarding hook is a single-use item used from the hotbar. *Proposal: no costume or undergarment stat slots, since the account wardrobe covers looks.*

### Market Isle (working name)

An instanced area that works like a big shop, like ArcheAge's Mirage Isle: the player goes in, shops in their own space and comes back to where they were. It sells designs (blueprints), not finished items.

* A design is consumed when crafting. **Gliders:** the design is used at a crafting table. **Ships:** the design opens a protected construction platform near the water, close to a port with a lighthouse; players add the resource packs, and if the construction isn't finished within about 4 h the platform disappears and the design returns; finishing it gives the item that summons the ship. **Houses:** the design becomes a construction on the player's own land.  
* Designs and everything built from them are bound and never tradeable; only the resources supplied for construction are tradeable.  
* Inventory expansions are paid with in-game gold, and the island can use special currencies. It never sells anything for real money.  
* Buying a house design doesn't give land: placing the house follows the land rules (8x8 slots, the limit per account and an active subscription).  
* It also sells home crafting-station designs and basement expansion designs (see [[Economy, housing and land]] › Houses).  
* The portal is free, since it isn't travel; it returns the player to the same spot and requires being idle.
