This is RunicGame's gameplay vision: it describes how the game should work, to guide the development of the engine, the platform and the game. It says what and why; the technical how lives in the RunicEngine documents.

* **Decisions** are in the body of each section and summarized in Recorded decisions, at the end.  
* **Numbers** (loads, percentages, ticks, chances, seconds) are examples that show the shape. Final values come from development and playtests.  
* **Proposals** not yet confirmed are marked as proposals; what still needs design is marked Open or being defined.  
* **Requirements for the engine and the platform** are collected in their own section, ready to become a technical backlog.  
* **Details** live in the appendices in the same folder: [[RunicGame — Appendix Nodes, Economy and Exploration|Nodes, Economy and Exploration]]; [[RunicGame — Appendix Content Pipeline and Art Direction|Content Pipeline and Art Direction]]; [[RunicGame — Quest Design|Quest Design]]; Lore; [[RunicGame — Appendix Ping Resolution|Ping Resolution]]; and the RunicEngine appendices (Security, [[RunicEngine — Appendix Database Layout|Database Layout]]). New ideas start in [[RunicGame — Ideias]].  
* **Names.** Every specific name in the game is in English, the first localization, since the game is global. Names are working names until a naming pass.
