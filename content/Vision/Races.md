Race is identity, lore and origin, with convenience traits only and nothing tied to combat. Any race can use any loadout and Discipline, so there is no race × class matrix to balance.

* **Five races**, decided on 27 October and replacing the three races of the earlier version: Humans and Dragonkin in Faction 1 (Continent 1), Elves and Orcs in Faction 2 (Continent 2), and the Undead (zombies and skeletons) in both factions.  
* **The Undead** rose when the Altars woke, and they choose a faction at the end of their chapter 1\. Where they start before choosing is open.  
* **One body:** every playable race shares one skeleton and one hitbox, so no race is short and PvP has no hitbox advantage.  
* Race sets the starting area, home city and first story chain. Each continent has two starting areas, one per race; which race starts where is open.  
* **Faction color:** faction is shown by UI color (an outline or the nameplate), not by race.  
* Pirate is a faction, not a race: any race can become a pirate. Changing faction keeps the race; in the lore the character becomes an exile and gets a reputation look.  
* Heritage visuals per race: cultural armor, mount and glider.  
* **Traits** will all be redefined; the old ones (longer breath, longer glides, less fall damage) belonged to the dropped races.  
* **Names:** Dragonkin is the final name (chosen over Dragonborn because of a trademark filing), with no wings. Orcs replace the beastfolk, who stay as an NPC people of the neutral continent.  
* **Dropped:** the sea, highland and rock peoples of the earlier version, and the deep people.
