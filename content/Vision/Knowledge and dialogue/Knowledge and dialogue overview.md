How players learn about the world: what a piece of knowledge is, who can tell it, how conversations reveal it, and where it is kept. Decided in November 2026. The lore itself lives in [[RunicGame — Lore]], and the quests that use it in [[RunicGame — Quest Design]].

### Principles

* **Diegetic.** Knowledge comes from the world: what NPCs say, what you show them, what you overhear, read, fight and gather. Some of it comes unprompted; the rest needs digging in conversation.  
* **For secrets, collections and some storylines.** Knowledge never gates gear, vendors or other services. The main storylines are easy to find; the secret ones can be hard.  
* **Never public.** Knowledge lives on the server, and a client only receives the entries its character has learned (see [[Knowledge entries#Visibility|Visibility]]).  
* **One system.** People, places, factions, gods, creatures, plants, recipes and drop rates are all knowledge, kept in one knowledge tab with its own sections (see [[Knowledge tab and bestiary]]).  
* **Written once, reused.** NPCs share layered dialogue templates and declare what they know with queries, so new knowledge never means rewriting hundreds of conversations.

### How a conversation works

1. The NPC opens with a line from its dialogue layers and may volunteer an entry, usually a Common one.  
2. Choices come from what the character already has: the NPC's role topics, follow-ups, leads, items to show and story beats. Choices give knowledge.  
3. Typing a word looks for a topic the NPC knows. A hit opens the topic and adds choices; a miss costs patience.  
4. Every answer is one of the results in [[NPC knowledge and trust#The answers|the answers]]: it tells, needs more trust, needs groundwork, refuses or doesn't know.  
5. Offering what you know raises your standing with that NPC, which opens the deeper answers.  
6. What you learn goes to the knowledge tab and lights up in the web of every NPC who holds it.
