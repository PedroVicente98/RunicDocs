RunicGame is a giant open-world MMORPG where exploring land, sea, sky and underground is the focus, with tactical action combat, named classes built from a weapon loadout and a Discipline, and a loop where faction PvP, group PvE and one world economy with regional trade feed each other.

**Design pillars**

1. **Real exploration in a dense world** — continents separated by seas full of islands, floating islands, the Sky Island and a full Underground, with everything interactable. Maps are merchandise, night is truly dark and even the most basic mob is dangerous.  
2. **Tactical, positional combat** — TERA-style spatial execution, ArcheAge-style skill interactions and a slower rhythm inspired by Crimson Desert, with long cooldowns and skills that matter.  
3. **You are what you hold** — at an Altar the player picks a weapon loadout and a Discipline, which together make a named class with a single role. Weapon skills are the basic kit, unlocked by level; skill points go into the Discipline.  
4. **PvE that matters** — dungeons, raids, naval raids and a boss region, with exclusive materials that feed the gear used in PvP.  
5. **Fair land and economy** — one world auction, regional trade through trade packs and nodes, and housing with no monopolies and no lost progress.  
6. **A clear subscription** — a subscription and no cash shop at all; the subscription gives land and offline labor, with no surprise limits. Whether the base game is free-to-play or buy-to-play is still open.  
7. **One world, the risk you choose** — PvE and PvP players share one continuous world and one economy, but never affect each other in open-world combat. All content is PvE, PvP or PvPvE. PvP is the default, PvE mode is always available, and the riskiest content pays more.  
8. **Play without obligation** — no dailies, login streaks or rewards that punish time away, and nobody loses anything personal by being away. Recurring content is weekly and community-driven, and its rewards are good but never necessary.

**What changes compared with ArcheAge**

| Problem in ArcheAge | RunicGame's answer |
| :---- | :---- |
| Land monopoly and unusable space between plots | An 8x8 slot grid, a land limit per account, and land that freezes instead of being lost |
| More than 120 classes from 3 free trees, hard to balance | 50 named classes from 10 weapon loadouts and 5 Disciplines, each with one role; the same points for everyone at max level |
| Little PvE and no raids | Dungeons, raids, naval raids and a boss region |
| Patreon doubled the maximum labor (5k to 10k) | The same maximum for everyone; only subscribers regenerate offline |
| Tab-target combat | Spatial action combat with an authoritative server |
