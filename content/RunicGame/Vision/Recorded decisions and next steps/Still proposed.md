* A soft flight ceiling.  
* Group and raid sizes.  
* The Balance Profile decided by the target of each hit.  
* Cloth with the longest iframe and plate the shortest.  
* The armor curve A ÷ (A \+ K).  
* Bosses with a Break bar instead of hard CC.  
* The peaks of Poison, Curse and Madness, and the 60% cap per skill on a bar.  
* The dodge shapes of Arcane and Occultism, carried over from the removed [[Weapon Class System#Disciplines|Disciplines]].  
* Defense's exclusive (absorb barriers, restoring [[Defense Evasion Guard and armor#Guard|Guard]] and reflect).  
* The cost of inventory expansions growing up to a maximum.  
* Language as a requirement to change faction.  
* Each current event defining from when it can be predicted.  
* Photo mode only out of combat.  
* [[Factions PvP and crime#Outlaw and Infamy|Outlaw]] staying on after death.  
* A chapter needing some of its region's arcs; chapter replays as [[Real exploration#Altars, death, respawn and teleports|Altar]] memories.  
* Four quest guidance levels.  
* The pack overriding the back slot without unequipping the glider.  
* Soul [[Runes|Runes]] as the name of the runes of living beings.
