1. An arena prototype with three [[Weapon Class System#Named classes|named classes]], one per role: Guardian (Sword \+ Shield \+ Defense, Tank), Lancer (Spear \+ War, Damage) and Priest (Wand \+ Focus \+ Vitality, Support), with the merged skill trees, Evasion, Guard per weapon, armor per piece, [[Combat#Resolve hard and light CC|Resolve]] and condition build-up.  
2. Implement the Skill API: components, timing profiles per weapon, interaction rules, condition build-up and PvE and PvP values, with the two-moment resolver on the server.  
3. Validate role efficiency, component coefficients and the build-up speeds with training dummies before building more [[Weapon Class System#Loadouts|loadouts]].  
4. A vertical slice of exploration: one region with night, light, regional mobs, [[Real exploration#Altars, death, respawn and teleports|Altars]], one node with charts and the map per Lineage.  
5. Design the mount and aerial trees and aerial combat before the flight zones.  
6. Write the first quests in the RunicWorld vault, with the Codex and the Chronicle (see [[Quest Design|RunicGame — Quest Design]]).
