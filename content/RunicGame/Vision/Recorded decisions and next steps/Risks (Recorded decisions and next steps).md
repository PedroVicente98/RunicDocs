* **Scope:** a giant, dense world, the Sky Island, naval and aerial combat and the boss region are a lot of content. Each system should start as a small vertical slice.  
* **Named classes:** 50 classes, each with mount and aerial trees, multiply the content and balance work; the merged trees and shared pools exist to keep it manageable, and launch can start with fewer.  
* **Barrier to entry:** hard mobs and dark nights can push new players away; the first region must teach blocking, dodging and light before punishing.  
* **Launch population:** whatever the base model, opening the servers needs monitoring to decide the pace.  
* **Writing cost:** a long story, a Codex and NPC knowledge profiles need cheap writing tools (taxonomy, role templates, coverage reports).
