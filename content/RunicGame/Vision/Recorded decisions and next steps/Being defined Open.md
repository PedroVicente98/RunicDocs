* The mount and aerial trees per class: balancing how easy it is to create new skills with the quality of aerial combat.  
* The base business model (free-to-play or buy-to-play) and, with it, the launch plan.  
* Defense's dodge shape and Vitality's exclusive; the Dual Axes' signature mechanic.  
* Where the Undead start before choosing a faction, which race starts in which area, and the new race traits.  
* The cooldown and requirements of switching between the PvE and PvP modes.  
* The [[Factions PvP and crime#Outlaw and Infamy|Infamy]] limit to become a pirate and the rate at which Infamy decays.  
* Whether pirates can return to a continental faction.  
* Whether picking up a dropped [[Trade packs and routes|trade pack]] from your own faction is a crime.  
* Whether a PvE mode player needs to switch to PvP to commit the crime required to change faction.  
* Whether profession proficiency is account-wide or per character.  
* Whether climbing mounts use [[Stamina and climbing#Stamina|stamina]].  
* The name of the runes of living beings, the special resource from coin purses, quest points and the [[Inventory equipment and the Market Isle#Market Isle (working name)|Market Isle]].  
* How story NPCs who die or turn traitor work in a shared world.
