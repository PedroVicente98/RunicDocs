Any weapon can reach the power ceiling, and that ceiling (BiS) is at most 10 to 20% above the weapon earned at the end of the main story. Raids and PvP shorten the path and give unique effects, but don't create a power gap: skill still matters more than gear.

## Anatomy of a weapon

| Part | What it does | How it evolves |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Weapon type | Belongs to a [[Weapon Class System#Loadouts\|loadout]] and defines its basic kit (Sword, Spear…) | Doesn't change |
| Item level | Base power | Deterministic upgrade with materials and gold |
| Grade | A small power bonus and visuals | Upgrade with a little RNG and a guarantee |
| Secondary stats | The weapon's profile (critical, speed, [[Defense Evasion Guard and armor#Guard\|Guard]], reach) | A reforge that shows 3 options and lets you keep the current one |
| Unique effect | Adds or swaps tags of a skill, changes the element, adds an effect or a new skill | Awakens at certain grades; can be extracted into a gem |
| Gem socket | Holds one gem with an Infusion Rune | A gem from extraction or dismantling goes in |

**Unique weapons.** Named weapons from specific bosses, PvP or rare recipes, with fixed secondary stats and a unique effect. Ex.: the Tide Spear, always Reach and Stagger; its effect makes the sweep pull targets. At the Epic and Mythic grades the effect awakens a second part and the visuals transform: a vertical upgrade, but in behavior, not in numbers.

**Unique effects and extraction**

* The effect of a unique weapon is flexible: it adds a tag to a skill, swaps tags, changes the element, adds an effect or adds a new skill.  
* Effects come from PvE and can also come from PvP; the ratio between the sources is a balance knob, and the system supports both. They work in PvE and PvP.  
* A weapon that adds a skill is stronger, and that is accepted.  
* **Extraction** is the same process as dismantling: it destroys the source item, and the effect becomes a gem. The gem holds an Infusion Rune; the rune is the item's descriptive flavor, with the same function. A gem goes into another weapon's socket (one per weapon), can be sold at the auction house, or can be crushed into another resource.

## Power budget

| Source of power | Range | Rule |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Campaign-end weapon | 100% | Reference |
| Item level up to the expansion cap | up to \+10% | No RNG |
| Grade up to Mythic | up to \+5% | Light RNG, with a guarantee |
| Unique effect (or its gem) | \+0% in numbers | Behavior, including a new skill; an extra skill makes the weapon stronger, and that is accepted |
| BiS (item level cap \+ Mythic) | about \+15.5% | Within the 10 to 20% target |

## Grades

The list of grades is data, not code: adding or removing a grade must not require a system change, and the ceiling stays the same.

| Grade | Cumulative bonus | Chance per attempt | Guarantee | Visuals |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Common | 0% | — | — | No glow |
| Uncommon | \+0.5% | 100% | — | No glow |
| Rare | \+1% | 100% | — | Light outline |
| Heroic | \+1.5% | 100% | — | Light outline |
| Epic | \+2.5% | 60% | On the 3rd attempt | Glow |
| Legendary | \+3.5% | 40% | On the 5th attempt | Particles |
| Celestial | \+4.5% | 30% | On the 6th attempt | Aura |
| Mythic | \+5% | 20% | On the 8th attempt | Visual transformation |

**RNG without oppression**

* Failing never destroys, breaks or downgrades the weapon.  
* A failure spends only half the materials and raises the chance of the next attempt.  
* The guarantee is shown in the interface: the worst case has a known cost.  
* Item level has no RNG; grade has a little; the stat reforge is a choice between options, not a bet.

**Mythic Spark** (working name): a rare material needed to raise grades above Epic (Legendary, Celestial and Mythic). It comes from raids, ranked PvP, world bosses and the boss region; dungeons only go up to Epic.

## Where everything comes from

| Source | Starting item level | Starting grade | Unique effects | Materials it gives |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| End of the main story | 100% | Rare | No | — |
| Dungeons | 102 to 104% | Rare or Heroic | Yes, one per boss | Item level and grades up to Epic |
| Raids | 105 to 108% | Epic | Yes, the most striking ones | Mythic Spark and high grades |
| PvP (honor and ranking) | 104 to 106% | Heroic | Yes; the PvE to PvP ratio is a balance knob | Mythic Spark and high grades |
| World bosses and the boss region | 105 to 108% | Epic | Yes | Mythic Spark and high grades |
| Crafting | 100 to 106%, by materials | Set by the recipe | From rare recipes | — (the item carries the crafter's name) |

**Why this encourages variety**

* Each dungeon and raid has its own unique effects; collecting effects for different builds makes players move through the content.  
* High-grade materials come from raids, ranked PvP and the boss region: any of them works, and doing several is the fastest path. An incentive, not an obligation.  
* Since every weapon reaches the ceiling, the weapon with the effect the player likes never becomes obsolete within the expansion.  
* New expansion: the ceiling rises, and percentage upgrades bring old weapons to the new base, not to the new BiS.

## Infinite progression

Vertical power stops at the expansion ceiling; what is infinite is horizontal and prestige. Progression is horizontal for most of the game, vertical only on gear.

| Track | How it repeats | Power ceiling |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Dungeon and raid ascensions | Each level adds rotating modifier modules (ex.: a rising tide, enemies that explode on death) | Gives grade materials, cosmetics and titles; never goes past the expansion ceiling |
| [[Real exploration#Exploration XP\|Exploration XP]] | Adventurer abilities, resources and [[Stamina and climbing#Stamina\|stamina]] in the exploration shop; never combat skills or cosmetics | Horizontal only |
| Regional renown | Reputation per region | Recipes, discounts and cosmetics |
| Expansions | New loadouts, [[Weapon Class System#Disciplines\|Disciplines]], regions and maps | A new item level ceiling |

New loadouts and Disciplines come in without rework: each brings its own skill pool, and role passives and item effects that read tags already work on them on launch day.
