Exploring happens on foot, mounted, with a glider, with a wing, in vehicles and by ship. Flight exists only on the neutral continent and the Sky Island; elsewhere you can only glide, and over the ocean only with the wind current.

* **Glider (ArcheAge style):** only glides. It is the first way to glide and uses the wind currents of the regions with that gimmick. Gliders are crafted at a table from a design.  
* **Wing:** equipment, equipped and not summoned. It glides like the glider and flies in the flight zones.  
* **Mounts with a function:** gliding and flying are separate capabilities, and a mount can have one, both or neither. There are also mounts that swim and mounts that climb. Function is what makes a mount desirable, not just speed.  
* **Stamina** is its own resource, physical effort in seconds and minutes, separate from labor. It is spent by sprinting, climbing, fast swimming, open-sea fishing and moving heavy objects; combat skills never cost stamina, and [[Defense Evasion Guard and armor#Guard|Guard]] and evasion have their own charges. In combat, costs are a fixed percentage of the maximum; out of combat, a flat value per tick. Regeneration is always a percentage of the maximum. The maximum is raised in the exploration shop.  
* **Climbing**, in the style of Crimson Desert: characters climb large mountains and cliffs with [[Stamina and climbing#Stamina|stamina]]. The terrain sets the drain by difficulty, and gear (spiked shoes, climbing gear) lowers it on the surfaces it covers. Some surfaces can never be climbed (castle and fort walls, barriers, zone boundaries, instance walls and house walls), and structures aren't climbable unless an artist opts them in. Getting hit while climbing removes a fixed part of the stamina. Pitons and ropes are consumables that help everyone on that stretch.  
* **Ship hulls** are climbed only with boarding hooks, which can be cut, the crew ropes every ship has, or movement skills.  
* **Vehicles:** the other vehicles are summoned by scroll. Flying vehicles work only in the flight zones. New vehicles take packs when they are built, except those made at a bench, such as gliders.  
* **Trade packs** don't let you fly or glide, only walk slowly. They need vehicles or specialized mounts; a mount carries one pack.  
* **Ships of several sizes** are essential for [[Trade packs and routes|trade packs]], housing islands and naval raids. Ships are built from designs on a construction platform.  
* **Context kit:** mounted, with a glider or with a wing, the character uses only the context skills (see the Weapon Class System).  
* **Between maps:** the island staircase of the neutral continent leads to the Sky Island; caves and rivers go down to the Underground, in the same map; other maps use portals.  
* **Every discrete action** (interactions, starting and stopping a sprint, using an item) has a minimum cooldown of 0.25 s, to prevent spam in a highly interactive world.
