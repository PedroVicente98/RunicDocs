In RunicGame's world, runes are intrinsic to magic and energy. So nothing is ever called just "Rune": every use carries a qualifier, in the documents and in the code.

| Name | What it is |
| :---- | :---- |
| World Runes | Lore objects; each can have its own raid |
| Runic Languages | The base of all magic |
| Runes of living beings (name open; *proposal: Soul Runes*) | They describe the life force and soul of each being |
| Infusion Rune | The rune held inside a gem made by extraction or dismantling; the gem goes into a weapon's socket (one per weapon). It is the gem's descriptive flavor, with no function of its own |

Names like Skill Rune or Exploration Rune are examples of the pattern, not decided systems. The special resource from coin purses is never a plain "Rune" either.
