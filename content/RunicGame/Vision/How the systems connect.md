No activity lives in isolation: PvE, PvP, the open world and [[Trade packs and routes|trade packs]] produce different resources that meet in endgame crafting, and the resulting gear goes back to the raids and the wars.

![[vision-fluxo-de-recursos-4-atividades-1-destino.png]]
*fluxo de recursos · 4 atividades, 1 destino*

*Resource flow · 4 activities, 1 destination (diagram from the 13 October version)*

Trade packs link the two halves: they carry the open world's production, create the PvP that pays honor and special resources, and build the regions through [[Regional economy#Prosperity and node missions|node missions]] and projects. Exploration feeds all of it with charts, routes and access to rare resources, and the world auction lets every player, PvE or PvP, sell what they make.
