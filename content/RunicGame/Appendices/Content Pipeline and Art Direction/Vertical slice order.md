**Proposal.** Each step is a good size for one slice:

* Recipes, the validation script and the naming conventions.
* The [[Characters|MPFB2]] bodies and one armor set made from the body.
* [[Animation|Quaternius]] animations retargeted in Godot.
* Trees merged into meshes per state and drawn with MultiMesh.
* One ship with its stations.
* The wolf and the goblin.

The Blender side can start before slices [[Slice 22 World rendering|22]] (world rendering) and 23 (animated characters) of the roadmap. Until [[GDB-13|GDB-13]], assets use Godot's standard material, which the proposed texture approach already fits.
