| **Vision section** | **What [[Vision\|the Vision]] says today** | **What this appendix changes** |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Lançamento, monetização, visuais e áudio › Visuais | Mount and pet collection, account-wide transmog wardrobe, weapon appearances per weapon type, exclusive cosmetics from dungeons and raids. No art direction | Adds an art direction (see Art direction). Transmog and weapon appearances are produced as variants of shared base models, and textures carry [[Characters\|dye masks]] from the start (Proposal) |
| Raças | Three races with heritage visuals: cultural armor, mount and glider | All playable races share one skeleton and one hurtbox, so every animation works on every race (Proposal). Heritage armor is a variant set built on the shared armor bases |
| Defesa: Evasão, [[Defense Evasion Guard and armor#Guard\|Guard]] e armaduras › Armaduras | Cloth, leather and plate change the iframe and the Guard | The same three classes are the three armor bases of the pipeline, one per body type (Decision). No armor stops the arm from going above the head (Proposal) |
| Skills por componentes › Atributos ocultos, públicos e perfis de tempo | Startup, active, recovery and cancel window in combat ticks (30 Hz); one timing profile per weapon and grip | Swing animations are authored in the same phases, and the pipeline checks each clip against its weapon's timing profile (Proposal) |
| O que a engine e a plataforma precisam suportar | No row for art or content production | Add a row for the content pipeline (see Requirements by repository) |
