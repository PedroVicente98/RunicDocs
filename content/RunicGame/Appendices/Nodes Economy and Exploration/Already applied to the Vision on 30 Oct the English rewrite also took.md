* Pillar 7, "One world, the risk you choose".  
* PvE and PvP participation modes, [[Factions PvP and crime#Jurisdiction|Jurisdiction]] and the "Who finds whom" table, with target discovery decided by the attacker.  
* [[Factions PvP and crime#Outlaw and Infamy|Outlaw]] and [[Factions PvP and crime#Outlaw and Infamy|Infamy]].  
* Balance Profile instead of "context".  
* Rune naming rule: nothing is ever just "Rune".  
* World bosses and the boss region as PvPvE.  
* The Skill API example now costs mana instead of [[Stamina and climbing#Stamina|stamina]].

Apart from the Skill API example, these topics are not yet in [[Architecture|Architecture v4]] (see the first row of the table above).
