| Vision section | What [[Vision\|the Vision]] said before 30 Oct | What this appendix changes |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Design pillars | Seven pillars | New pillar 8, "Play without obligation": no dailies, login streaks or losses for being away; recurring content is weekly and community-driven |
| Pillar 6, Subscription, Regional economy ([[Labor\|labor]]) | Same maximum labor for everyone; only subscribers regenerate offline; labor is character state | Labor is account-wide. Labor reserve: with a full bar, offline regeneration fills a reserve of up to 1× the maximum that never expires. The reserve is for subscribers only, following the existing offline rule |
| Real exploration: Maps as a resource | Three map types (city map sold by NPCs, exploration map, nautical chart) per region. Cartography records what the player travels, and a Carta is more complete and valuable the more was traveled | Three map detail levels (fog, discovered, mapped). Charts per node and per layer (base, resources, fauna, events, environment); no map holds everything. [[Maps and cartography#Surveying\|Surveying]] is a PvP minigame with its own tools ([[Maps and cartography#Triangulation (base chart on land)\|triangulation]] on land, sextant at sea). A finished chart has no grade: placement quality changes time and labor. The player always sees their exact position; a node's chart adds the node's information. Charts unlock in-game interactions. Starter areas come surveyed, with charts for sale. A chart is consumed when used |
| Real exploration: [[Real exploration#Exploration XP\|Exploration XP]] | Exploration shop with adventurer abilities or resources | The shop also sells [[Stamina and climbing#Stamina\|stamina]] upgrades. Items found on summits are one source of the shop's currency. Exploration discoveries cost labor and belong to Cartography, whose labor they use |
| Real exploration: Professions | Jeweler, leather, blacksmith, fishing, farming, animal husbandry, cartography, nautical charts and language | New rule: every labor cost belongs to a profession. List: the 23 professions based on ArcheAge's (in the Vision since 30 Oct), with coin purses in Larceny and exploration in Cartography. Farming and husbandry stay professions. Profession gear in its own always-active slots; minigames with capped bonuses and a batch mode |
| Real exploration: [[Real exploration#Altars, death, respawn and teleports\|Altars]] | Proposal: Altars do not teleport between each other | Changed: settlement Altars are teleport destinations (the Lineage sees them on the map, but respawn and teleports use only the Altars each character has discovered); only when idle; a fixed fee plus distance, more between continents; from the field also a consumable and a long cooldown; no cargo fee; fixed world portals with a toll |
| Factions: Participation modes | Table "Base map cartography: PvE yes"; "PvE mode makes base maps" | Surveying is PvP, so PvE mode does not create base maps: PvE players buy charts |
| Recorded decisions: Castles | One castle per region, built by the dominant guild | A region is a city node with its child nodes. A guild holds up to 3 nodes or one region, never both. Castles exist only on the neutral continent; on the faction continents the ruling guild uses the city and an exclusive guild building. Guild income is a fixed weekly payout |
| Economy, housing and land: Regional economy | Specialty per region; regional auction houses | Specialty per node and per region, with material-return bonuses; faction capitals specialize in finished products, goods and equipment; one global auction, with regional price movement in trade-pack demand; [[Regional economy#Prosperity and node missions\|prosperity]] 1 to 5 through weekly [[Regional economy#Prosperity and node missions\|node missions]]; one global chest; mail with postage per unit |
| Economy, housing and land: tax | "Tax" is the weekly land tax paid from the tax fund | The auction tax, paid by the seller and kept entirely by the NPC as a sink, is separate from the weekly land tax. Guilds get a fixed weekly payout instead of a tax share |
| Regional economy: [[Trade packs and routes\|trade packs]] | Carry a region's production to another and pay gold and special resources | Pack types copied from ArcheAge (specialty, aged, cargo, resource, military aid). Specialty payout follows the destination region's demand; resource packs build and maintain the regions. The route service and the origin node stay |
| Inventory and [[Inventory equipment and the Market Isle#Market Isle (working name)\|Market Isle]] | Instanced shop selling inventory expansions, furniture, houses, vehicles and gliders; currency for non-inventory items undefined | The Market Isle sells designs (blueprints), not finished items. Gliders are crafted at a table; a ship design opens a protected construction platform near a lighthouse port; a house design becomes a construction on the player's land. Designs and everything built from them are bound and never tradeable; special currencies are allowed. The portal is free and requires being idle |
| Movement and vehicles | Mounts that climb; no character climbing | Character climbing with stamina, difficulty per surface, gear and never-climbable surfaces. Ship hulls are climbed only with boarding hooks, the ship's crew ropes or movement skills. Open: whether climbing mounts use stamina |
| Defense: [[Defense Evasion Guard and armor#Guard\|Guard]] per weapon | Spear: "sprint with frontal Guard" | Sprinting now costs stamina. The Spear's sprint with Guard is a separate skill and costs no stamina |
| Races | Sea folk: more breath and faster swimming | Compatible: breath stays separate from stamina, and the trait changes base swim speed, not the cost of fast swimming |
| Recorded decisions: Weapon swap | Weapon swap only out of combat | Unchanged. Profession gear lives in separate slots that are always active, so there is no [[Weapon Class System#Loadouts\|loadout]] swap at all |
| Combat (new rule) | No definition of combat state | Combat starts on the first hostile action and ends 30 s after the last one |
| Infinite progression | Vertical power stops at the expansion cap; what is infinite is horizontal | Compatible and reinforced: progression is horizontal for most of the game, vertical only on gear |
| Housing: crops and animals | Crops keep their state when land freezes | Ready crops never wither. Neglected animals get sick but never die, unless the player slaughters them for meat |
| What the engine and platform must support | Rows for Maps, Progression, Housing and economy, and Platform with the old rules | Update those rows and add Nodes, Stamina and climbing, Profession gear and Actions (see Requirements by repository) |
| Recorded decisions, Still proposed, Open | None of this appendix's topics | Add the decisions and open items listed here |
