* **Decision.** A character enters combat on the first hostile action, immediately.  
* **Decision.** Combat ends 30 s after the last hostile action.  
* Combat state gates teleports (a teleport needs the character idle, which includes out of combat) and switches [[Stamina and climbing#Stamina|stamina]] costs to the in-combat mode.
