**ID:** LORE-04 · **Status:** Open

The main story is mandatory, long and non-intrusive ([[QUEST-02 Main Story|QUEST-02]]). The rest is optional side content in contained quests, because many players don't care about story, and the world's history adds immersion without holding anyone back. The world should feel like it is expanding ([[CITY-01 Settlements and Neutral Cities|CITY-01]]).

The universe's lore is written for the game, with no existing IP and no books, series or films outside it. Players who want to read find the stories in the world, as in-game books like Skyrim's. Books are optional side content, and players can keep them in a library at home.
