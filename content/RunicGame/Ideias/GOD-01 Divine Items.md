**ID:** GOD-01 · **Status:** Open

Completely broken single-use items tied to the gods, causing a cataclysmic event that turns a battle. Ex.: a fire god scroll that rains meteors over the whole battlefield; a sea god orb that opens a whirlpool and summons a horde against the enemies. Some are chaotic and neutral: they hit everyone, the user included. The game doesn't have to be 100% competitive or serious.

Each one is an item infused with a god's power through a long and difficult process. Extremely hard to get: a large guild works for months for one. Always single use, never permanent. Using one is a divine ritual ([[RITUAL-01 Rituals|RITUAL-01]]): 5 people and 30 s to 1 min of cast, with a very big animation. Usable at any time, except in instanced places. Tradeable, with very high costs.

The divine effects are also world events. Admins can trigger them as a mediation tool: if a guild starts to build a monopoly, they raise the chance of these events. The events can also happen naturally, with no one's input, but very rarely. Each effect has the region types where it can happen (water or land, mountain or forest, and others). The events also hit players in PvE mode, and they damage castles and constructions, except those on players' land.

*(proposal: the chance rises by a public rule, ex.: with the number of nodes a guild holds, and the lore presents it as the gods punishing excess, so it reads as a world rule and not as admin favoritism; admins tune the rule and announce manual events. Each effect declares where it can happen from the node's climate and environment, and the living-world service rolls per node per cycle, ex.: 200 nodes with a 0.1% chance per node per week give about 0.2 events a week in the whole world, one every 5 weeks. A natural event is announced minutes before it hits, and a master cartographer can predict it like current events, [[INTEL-01 Resource Richness and Perishable Information|INTEL-01]].)*

*(proposal: a long, visible telegraph, now the divine ritual in [[RITUAL-01 Rituals|RITUAL-01]], so it becomes a spectacle with counterplay, interrupting or fleeing; on the server, hazard zones at intervals (cast shapes), not physics objects; a multi-stage recipe from raids, world bosses, high-level rifts and several professions; the neutral items are infused with the power of Chaos.)*

Divine items can carry NPC conditions ([[BUILD-01 Conditions Build-up Resolve and Tolerance|BUILD-01]]). Ex.: Rain of Death applies Instant Death build-up to everyone in the area, its user included, since divine effects are neutral. It isn't an instant wipe: it turns the area into a clock that players fight with cleanses or by leaving. *(proposal: it also hits creatures in the area, and world bosses resist it.)*
