**ID:** MOV-02 · **Status:** Open

Mounts, ships and gliders should go deeper than ArcheAge; flight is out of scope for now. *The rules below are proposals.*

* **Gliders:** landing without free fall (a flare landing near the ground, and stowing at low height with no fall damage; during lag, the server keeps gliding on the last input). Stats (glide ratio, speed, turn rate, stall), thermals, glider kit skills, and parts from different professions.  
* **Mounts:** raised from a foal through a quest, husbandry breeding with inherited traits, mount gear from other professions (saddle from leatherwork, barding from metalwork), and knocked out instead of dead.  
* **Ships:** modular parts (hull, bow, masts, sails, rigging, helm, cannons, figurehead) from different professions, upgraded part by part, each ship type allowing its own upgrades (ex.: an ice bow, a speed bow or a ram bow that deals more collision damage), and a sail crest editor with layered symbols instead of image uploads.

**Taming:** wild mounts can't be tamed. Some wild creatures may have a baby version that can be tamed and then raised with the same system as the quest mount.
