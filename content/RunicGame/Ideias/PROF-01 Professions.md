**ID:** PROF-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Real exploration#Professions|Real exploration › Professions]]

Professions level up with practice. There are 23, based on ArcheAge's list ([[PROF-02 Professions and Masteries|PROF-02]]): Cartography includes nautical charts and exploration, and Larceny includes opening coin purses.

Language lets you talk with the other faction *(proposal: it is a requirement to change faction)*. Cartography makes the charts sold at the auction house (see [[MAP-01 Maps as a Resource|MAP-01]]).

*Updated 2026-10-30: 23 professions (was: jeweler, leather, blacksmith, fishing, farming, animal husbandry, cartography, nautical charts and language).*
