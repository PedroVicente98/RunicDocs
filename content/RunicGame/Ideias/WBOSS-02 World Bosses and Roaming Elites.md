**ID:** WBOSS-02 · **Status:** Open

Big monsters live in the open world, on land and in the ocean, in two kinds:

* **World bosses:** completely unique and impossible to solo. Ex.: giant sea monsters like a leviathan or a kraken that need about 100 players on several ships, with a chance of a legendary item (inspired by ArcheAge's ocean). Long respawns, the best gear and prestige.  
* **Roaming elites:** rare, bigger and stronger versions of normal monsters. They aren't unique, only rarer. Hard to solo, but possible.

*(proposal: roaming elites wander on routes from the living-world schedule ([[LIVE-01 Living World|LIVE-01]]), show up in the fauna chart as rare spawns ([[INTEL-01 Resource Richness and Perishable Information|INTEL-01]]) and have their own bestiary entries ([[BEST-01 Bestiary|BEST-01]]).)*
