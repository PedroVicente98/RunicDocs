**ID:** QUEST-02 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Quests and story#Main story|Quests and story › Main story]]

The main story is mandatory, long and non-intrusive, with branching choices ([[QUEST-01 Quests and Leveling Content|QUEST-01]]): it runs beside the rest of the leveling and never gets in the way. The player shapes the rest of the leveling by choosing side content, which also teaches the game's systems ([[LVL-01 Leveling|LVL-01]]).

* **Chapters:** chapter 1 is per race, in the race's starter area, and every branch merges at the faction's capital. From chapter 2 on, each faction has its own chapters, and they mirror each other: the same story about the world and the war, told from each side, without diverging much. Choices in earlier chapters can change the branches of later ones.  
* **Completion:** each chapter keeps all its possible branches, so a player can replay it and see what the character chose, for completionists.  
* **Presentation:** text with still images, like ArcheAge's. No voice acting or narration unless the game gets large funding.  
* **Side content:** books and profession quest lines are optional.  
* **Regional arcs:** each region the story reaches has its own contained arc with branches, which is part of the main story. The player makes a choice and moves on to the next region.  
* **Story missions:** engaging activities, like a dungeon or an event, never quests to kill 50 mobs. Killing mobs only happens as part of a more interesting activity.  
* **Skippable text:** all story text can be skipped, for players who only want the playable content.  
* **Alts:** a Lineage can skip the chapters it has already played, so alts don't repeat the story. Still open: how many characters must finish it first, and whether each faction's and each race's chapters unlock separately.

**Open:** whether replaying a chapter changes the character's choices or only fills the completion record, and whether completion is tracked per character or per Lineage. *(proposal: a replay is a memory relived at an [[Real exploration#Altars, death, respawn and teleports|Altar]], which fills the record without changing the character's choices; the record of seen branches is per Lineage, like achievements in [[FAM-01 Account and Alts and The Family|FAM-01]].)*

*Updated 2026-10-30: the main story is long and non-intrusive (was: short and never repetitive, so it doesn't overstay).*
