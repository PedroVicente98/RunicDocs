**ID:** WBOSS-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Open world|Open world]] (the boss region)

A region that is a world boss, so every thing done in the region is progression toward the fight, from the summoning by progressing the region via interactions from mob farming, or resource gathering, to then the boss fight taking the whole region, all factions (pirates included) earn contribution, its per individual but also faction and guild, so many rankings, rewards based on contribution fix rewards and luck RNG scale with contribution, and PVP steal contribution

*Updated 2026-10-16: all factions, pirates included (was: both factions earn contribution).*
