**ID:** DISC-03 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Weapon Class System#Skills, trees and points|Weapon Class System › Skills, trees and points]]

A [[Weapon Class System#Disciplines|Discipline]] is a pool of skills, a mask over the kit. Each Discipline has a tree of about 15 skills, but its full pool is larger: it includes skills exclusive to a weapon type (instrument, staff, shield…) and skills shared with other pools. A [[Weapon Class System#Named classes|named class]]'s single tree is the merge of the weapon and Discipline skills whose requirements match the equipped weapons; a skill in several pools appears once. The design work is to write many skills per weapon and per Discipline, then match the compatible combinations.

Example: War, Vitality or Defense combined with Sword \+ Shield, Staff or Lute. Each combination is a named class with its own skills. A Flute with a damage Discipline is a flavor of mage that damages with sound.

* **Weapon skills** are the basic kit: light, heavy, the off-hand action and the cheap ranged strike, always available. More unlock automatically by level, with no point cost. Some levels offer a choice (ex.: level 35: flame, ice or lightning strike), changed only at the [[Real exploration#Altars, death, respawn and teleports|Altar]] reset.  
* **Discipline skills** are the main kit. They cost skill points and are tiered: a pick in one tier unlocks the next.  
* **Values per class:** the same skill can scale differently in different classes.

*(proposal: requirement tags on each skill, ex.: needs an instrument in either hand; the named-class list is the allowlist; trees generated at cook time, with nodes pinned, removed or moved by hand per class; the most specific value wins, class \+ PvP over class, over PvP, over base; per-class overrides stay exceptions.)*
