**ID:** TRANSIT-01 · **Status:** Open

Deeper than ArcheAge's, and without its lag-spike deaths. The article's complaints: carriages are too fast to catch and too slow to wait for, often going the wrong way, with long gaps; on airships, a lag spike made the player fall and lose the [[Trade packs and routes|trade pack]]. *The rules below are proposals.*

* **Schedules:** lines run on the living-world schedule ([[LIVE-01 Living World|LIVE-01]]), so vehicles only exist near players. Frequency \= loop time ÷ vehicles: a 30-minute loop with 6 carriages gives one every 5 minutes each way, a 2.5-minute average wait.  
* **Boarding:** vehicles wait at stations and ring a bell before leaving, timetables are posted at stations, and you can hail a carriage from the roadside.  
* **Trade packs:** allowed, but lines run through safe nodes, so the route service pays less ([[TRADE-01 Trade Packs|TRADE-01]]).  
* **Lag:** while riding, the server stores the player in the vehicle's local coordinates. A missing input never detaches you; only dismounting, jumping off or being knocked off does. The attachment state goes into the [[Networking replication interest and handoff#Simulations, handoff and ghosts|handoff]] snapshot.
