**ID:** PRICE-01 · **Status:** Partial · **Game vision:** [[Economy housing and land|Economy, housing and land › The world economy]] (price bands)

Fixes the high prices that the Fanatical Swordsman article criticized in ArcheAge's crafting. The article's other pros stay: interdependence between professions, [[Trade packs and routes|trade packs]] that consume materials and create PvP, and specialization through limits.

* **Price bands:** every item has a minimum and a maximum price. When an item is out of stock or the market goes above the maximum, an NPC listing appears and sells at a high price, which caps the market. *(proposal: bands derived from the global auction's rolling average and recalculated weekly, wide enough to rarely bind; the NPC listing avoids the queues and lotteries of Black Desert's central market.)*  
* **2+ sources:** every non-craftable material has two or more ways to get it, with different efficiency (ex.: a fast, risky PvP path and a slower, safe PvE path). A special tree, for example, comes from farming, from a fixed dungeon boss at 2% and at 100% at the end of a rift of that theme ([[RIFT-01 Chaos Rifts|RIFT-01]]).

**Not in [[Vision|the game vision]] yet:** 2+ sources for every non-craftable material.
