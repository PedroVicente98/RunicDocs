**ID:** LIVE-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Open world|Open world]] (living world)

Escorts, trade routes, ships and mob routines follow schedules mapped to world time, without simulating empty areas: the NPC shows up at the right point when a player arrives. The routine drifts slowly (a cycle that doesn’t match the clock, plus a small deterministic variation). Exceptions caused by players, like a delayed escort, are stored in a service and return to the routine over time.
