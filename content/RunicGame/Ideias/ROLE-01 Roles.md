**ID:** ROLE-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Weapon Class System#Roles|Weapon Class System › Roles]]

The role comes from the named class: each loadout \+ [[Weapon Class System#Disciplines|Discipline]] combination has one role (Damage, Tank or Support), set in the class design, in similar numbers: 18 Damage, 15 Tank and 17 Support among the 50 classes. There is no separate role choice. The role sets the ceiling of what the class does well, the role kit and passive, and the dodge charges.

Role kit examples: Tank has Taunt (PvE), Challenge with area protection (PvP) and Unbreakable [[Defense Evasion Guard and armor#Guard|Guard]]; Support has heals and buffs with FriendlyLock, resurrection and cleanse; Damage has execution and a burst window.

Efficiency per role applies to everything the character produces, starting values for playtest: Tank 55% damage, 45% healing, 100% mitigation, 70% buffs; Support 45%, 100%, 50%, 85%; Damage 100%, 35%, 40%, 70%.

When the role passive and a Discipline effect give the same effect, they don’t stack: the strongest wins (provisional).

Each [[Weapon Class System#Named classes|named class]] also has a small signature effect, the per-class balance lever, used sparingly.

*Updated 2026-10-30: the roles are Damage, Tank and Support (was: Tank, Healer and DPS).*
