**ID:** CAPRAID-01 · **Status:** Open

Like WoW: the enemy faction can invade a capital, kill its NPCs and fight the faction leader, a very hard boss, in what works as a raid. Killed NPCs take a while to respawn ([[LORE-02 Why Adventurers Come Back|LORE-02]]). Guards are very strong, with more attack and damage, so the whole city plays like a raid where everyone must play well. Non-combat NPCs, like the banker, don't attack.

*(proposal: essential services, the bank, mail, auction house and [[Real exploration#Altars, death, respawn and teleports|Altar]], can't be killed, so an invasion isn't grief; rewards are honor and titles, not materials, so it isn't farmed; the capital runs on its own simulation, as already decided for crowded areas.)*
