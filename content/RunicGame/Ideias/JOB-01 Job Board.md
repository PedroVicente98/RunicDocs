**ID:** JOB-01 · **Status:** Open

A community board where players hire each other, the main tool for the rule that nobody is the best at everything ([[PROF-02 Professions and Masteries|PROF-02]]). References: ArcheAge Unchained's crafting request board, WoW crafting orders and EVE Online courier contracts. *The rules below are proposals.*

* **Local:** one board per city or node, and deliveries land in the global chest. *(proposal: transport jobs carry only [[Trade packs and routes|trade packs]], since other goods move through the auction.)*  
* **Escrow:** payment is locked when the job is posted. Transport jobs also lock collateral from the courier.  
  * Internal bank in the job board: a player can take a job without having the gold for the collateral. If they fail the job and can't pay, their balance goes negative, and they can't take other jobs until they pay it back.  
  * Cancel and failure: a player can cancel a job and return everything, and nobody loses. Failing means not delivering by the deadline without cancelling, and only then is the collateral paid. In practice, the job board works as a lending system.  
  * *(proposal: a small fee on every contract funds a pool that pays the client when a debt isn't paid, and repaid debts go back to the pool, so an unpaid debt doesn't create gold; the debt belongs to the Lineage, and the credit limit grows with the contract history; goods in a transport job are sealed and can only be delivered or returned, so failing costs the client only time; cancelling happens out of combat, a carried trade pack can only be cancelled at a job board, and a trade pack lost in PvP counts as a failure.)*  
* **Contract types:** crafting order (materials from the client or the crafter), [[Labor|labor]] service (spend your labor on my materials), gathering order, transport or escort, survey commission, fragment deciphering ([[SITE-01 Map Fragments and Sites|SITE-01]]), ship construction help and sport-fishing charter.  
* **Reputation:** the crafter's mark on items and a history of completed contracts.  
* **No FOMO:** contracts expire with a full refund, and there is no daily board.  
* **Modes:** crafting and gathering contracts also work in PvE mode; carrying trade packs stays PvP.
