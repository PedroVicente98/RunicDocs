**ID:** COMM-01 · **Status:** Open

Everything about the economy and prices belongs to Commerce. Buying and selling at the auction house never costs [[Labor|labor]].

Trade packs cost labor to craft and to deliver, at a ratio of about 1 to 0.75 (ex.: 40 to craft and 30 to deliver; the values aren't final). Commerce proficiency lowers the cost and improves the payout. In ArcheAge, crafting a pack cost labor reduced by Commerce, and buying Cargo later cost 75 labor.

Demand reports (the pack demand of each region, the market's stock chart) are Commerce products and go stale like the charts in [[INTEL-01 Resource Richness and Perishable Information|INTEL-01]]; the board in each city shows only its own region. Cargo packs are consolidated through Commerce and pay more the faster they arrive ([[TRADE-01 Trade Packs|TRADE-01]]).

**Open:** which other actions belong to Commerce. *(proposal, candidates: market stalls in cities, whose setup and restock cost labor; NPC bulk orders with haggling; contraband packs for pirates.)*
