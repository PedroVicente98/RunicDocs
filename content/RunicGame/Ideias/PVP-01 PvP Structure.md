**ID:** PVP-01 · **Status:** Open

* **Faction vs faction:** a region-control event between factions, like Guild Wars 2's World vs World and Elder Scrolls Online's Cyrodiil, but never instanced. The event gives the region special rules: only players taking part can fight each other, so nobody outside it can interfere.  
* **Guild vs guild:** node and castle wars. Cities are nodes, so this also covers sieges for city control, like Black Desert's. It is plain open-world PvP, with all its chaos.  
* **Open-world PvP:** trade runs, attacks on PvE caravans and their interception, world bosses and many other activities.  
* **PvP instances:** battlegrounds and arenas together, as instances with different rules and sizes, from 1v1, 2v2, 3v3 and 5v5 to larger modes (inspired by WoW's battlegrounds and arenas, Star Wars: The Old Republic's Huttball and Blade & Soul's 1v1). They can have a ranked mode.  
* **Town arenas:** an area inside a city where PvP is allowed only within the arena, like Black Desert's.

*(proposal: event participation is a tag read by the attacker-side target discovery, the same filter that keeps PvE and PvP players apart.)*
