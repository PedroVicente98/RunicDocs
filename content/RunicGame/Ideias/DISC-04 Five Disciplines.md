**ID:** DISC-04 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Weapon Class System#Disciplines|Weapon Class System › Disciplines]]

Five [[Weapon Class System#Disciplines|Disciplines]] at launch: War, Defense, Vitality, Arcane and Occultism (Guerra, Vigília, Restauração, Arcano and Ocultismo). Sombra and Elementalismo are removed: Arcane takes the elemental spells, and Occultism takes Sombra's stealth and teleports.

* **War:** physical techniques and Fury, with damage and life steal. Always Damage. Exclusive: Bleed ([[BUILD-01 Conditions Build-up Resolve and Tolerance|BUILD-01]]).  
* **Defense:** always Tank, except on the Bow, where it's Support: shield arrows damage the enemy and shield allies. The Vigilant shields more and the Sacred Archer (Vitality) heals more. Mages tank with magic shields (Wand \+ Focus, Staff), and the Instrument with magic shields around it.  
* **Vitality:** heals, buffs and cleanse, the largest support kit. Always Support.  
* **Arcane:** rune magic. Raw arcane damage that ignores part of magical armor ([[DMG-01 Damage Types|DMG-01]]), plus elemental spells like fireball and meteor, which build Burn, Chill and Shock. Exclusive: arcane damage, with no build-up, and Acid ([[BUILD-01 Conditions Build-up Resolve and Tolerance|BUILD-01]]). Damage or Tank by [[Weapon Class System#Loadouts|loadout]].  
* **Occultism:** skills paid with life, stealth and teleports. Exclusive: Curse and Madness ([[BUILD-01 Conditions Build-up Resolve and Tolerance|BUILD-01]]). Damage, Tank or Support by loadout.

Discipline skills are filtered by the weapon: a skill that needs a shield is shared by Sword \+ Shield and Wand \+ Shield ([[DISC-03 Disciplines as Skill Pools|DISC-03]]).

Elemental conditions (Burn, Chill, Shock, Poison) are open to every Discipline, because elemental is too wide to lock into one; each Discipline also has something exclusive. *(proposal: Defense's exclusive is absorb barriers, restoring [[Defense Evasion Guard and armor#Guard|Guard]] and reflect.)*
