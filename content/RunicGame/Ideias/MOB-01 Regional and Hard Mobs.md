**ID:** MOB-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Real exploration#Regional and hard mobs|Real exploration › Regional and hard mobs]]

Even the most basic mob has telegraphs, blocks and positions itself; nothing dies by just pressing a button. Each region has its own threats on land, in the air and in the water, tied to its gimmick.

Land: pack predators and ambushes on trails. Air: raptors and wyverns that knock down whoever is gliding. Water: sharks, reef creatures and sea monsters.
