**ID:** MAP-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Real exploration#Maps as a resource|Real exploration › Maps as a resource]]

Map discovery belongs to the Lineage and is shared by every character ([[FAM-01 Account and Alts and The Family|FAM-01]]). The map starts in fog, becomes a static image where any character of the Lineage has walked, and becomes a real-time map with the node's base chart. Charts are sold per node and per layer (base, resources, fauna, events and environment). Details: [[Nodes Economy and Exploration|RunicGame — Appendix: Nodes, Economy and Exploration]].

Charts are player products, sold at the auction house and directly; starter areas come surveyed, with charts for sale, so every player learns the mechanic. PvE players buy charts.

Cartography is a profession, with nautical charts and exploration in it. Surveying is a PvP activity, with its own tools and minigame per layer (triangulation on land, a sextant and soundings at sea, drift buoys, a spyglass for fauna, samples for resources). Copies cost paper, ink and [[Labor|labor]].

Reading a chart unlocks it for the Lineage and consumes it. Regions whose gimmick changes the terrain can get new editions each season.

Current charts: with high proficiency the cartographer syncs the chart with the current state; at max level they predict events.

*Updated 2026-10-30: map discovery per Lineage, with charts per node and per layer (was: a map per character; city and road maps sold by NPC cartographers, exploration maps and nautical charts made by players; a cartography kit that records what you travel; a Map that reveals the region for the character who reads it).*
