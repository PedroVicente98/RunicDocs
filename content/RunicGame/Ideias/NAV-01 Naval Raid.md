**ID:** NAV-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[PvE|PvE]] (naval raid rules)

The Open World will already have naval combat and plenty of content in the water, but the raids also benefit from some naval combat. Raids needs many people, so they can fill 2+ ships to battle a water boss in a instanced map, can be underground, or just a segment of open world that is instanced. Limit Number of Ships in instance so the raid gives a ship to the player, the player can use the raid ship or its own, but not both. Raid ship expire after the raid.
