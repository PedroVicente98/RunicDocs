**ID:** SOC-01 · **Status:** Open

A large emote system and in-game marriage for roleplay, inspired by Final Fantasy XIV.

**Marriage** is per character and works as a stricter family: only its name can be edited, and members can't be added or removed. Players of different factions can't marry. The couple shares permissions on each other's houses, farms, ships and chests, under the per-character rule in [[FAM-02 Lineage and Family|FAM-02]], and each item can still be set to private ([[XFER-01 Transfers Between Accounts|XFER-01]]). Each Lineage keeps its own land limit, and the couple's name doesn't change either Lineage's name. Marriage gives the couple no gameplay bonus. The rings show each partner's exact position on the map while both wear them; they don't teleport. Trading through shared chests is covered in [[XFER-01 Transfers Between Accounts|XFER-01]].

**Ceremony:** required, at an [[Real exploration#Altars, death, respawn and teleports|Altar]] with guests. Everything in it is made by players, who do it themselves, get help or hire through the job board ([[JOB-01 Job Board|JOB-01]]). The minimum is the official papers (Printing), the rings (Handicrafts) and a priest. Optional extras: flowers (Gathering), decorations (Leatherwork, Tailoring and others) and food (Cooking).

**Guests:** attending gives a small buff for some hours. The ceremony food gives buffs too, stronger the more the couple spent on the ceremony.

**Divorce** goes through an NPC priest, who hands over legal documents to deliver to another building. One player alone can do it, and it takes effect after about 48 hours; both players together make it instant. Divorce also has a cost and a flag, and isn't possible in the first month or so after the wedding. If exploits appear, married couples get specific balancing nerfs.

**Open:** whether the priest is an NPC, a player or both, and whether a player priest is hired through the job board.

The two characters must be from different Lineages. Only one character per account can be online at a time, so marrying your own alts is already impossible, but the rule stays.

The attendance buff has a cooldown, so nobody hops from wedding to wedding for it.

The 48 hours of a one-sided divorce give the other partner time to reclaim items. *(proposal: during those 48 hours, each partner can take out only the items they deposited, so neither can use the window to empty the other's chests.)*

A married character can also be in one regular family ([[FAM-02 Lineage and Family|FAM-02]]).
