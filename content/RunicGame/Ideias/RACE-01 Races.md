**ID:** RACE-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Races|Races]]

Race is identity, lore and origin, with convenience abilities and nothing tied to combat. Any race can use any loadout and [[Weapon Class System#Disciplines|Discipline]], so there is no race × class matrix to balance.

Five races: Humans and Dragonkin in Faction 1 (Continent 1), Elves and Orcs in Faction 2 (Continent 2), and the Undead in both factions, who choose a faction at the end of their chapter 1\. Race defines the starting area, home city and first story chain. Every playable race shares one skeleton and one hitbox.

Pirate is a faction, not a race: any race can become a pirate. Changing faction keeps the race; in the lore the character becomes an exile and gets a reputation look.

Heritage visuals per race: cultural armor, mount and glider.

Traits will all be redefined; the old ones (longer breath, longer glides, less fall damage) belonged to the dropped races. Details and the NPC peoples: [[Lore|RunicGame — Lore]].

*Updated 2026-10-30: five races from RunicGame — Lore and [[Quest Design|RunicGame — Quest Design]] (was: 3 races, the sea, highland and rock peoples with their traits; any Vertente, weapon and Discipline).*
