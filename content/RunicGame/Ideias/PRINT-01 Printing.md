**ID:** PRINT-01 · **Status:** Open

Printing makes paper, special papers and other content used in the game outside Cartography. It stays a strong profession, without ArcheAge's regrade scrolls or similar endgame items. In ArcheAge, Printing made paper, books, regrade scrolls and spellbooks, and the regrade scrolls made it matter because every player upgrading gear needed them.

* Paper comes from wood. Special papers need the Printing mastery, ex.: bestiary paper ([[BEST-01 Bestiary|BEST-01]]), music paper for sheets ([[ART-01 Instruments Performance and Artistry|ART-01]]) and the special paper for tier 4 site maps ([[SITE-01 Map Fragments and Sites|SITE-01]]).  
* Ink comes from Alchemy and needs no mastery.  
* Cartographers copy their own charts with Printing's paper, Alchemy's ink and their own [[Labor|labor]].

**Open:** special magical books that make books valuable.
