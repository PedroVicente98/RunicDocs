**ID:** LVL-01 · **Status:** Open

The leveling experience need to be meaningful, the game cant rush you to the endgame content. I want the levelling to introduce all parts of the endgame in slices so players learn at their own pace, and aren't completely taken back by some new system the endgame introduces. Also levelling should be profitable, so making alts and levelling, although not the most profitable activity, cant feel like a waste of time. People who just like the levelling process can create many alts and gain money like that.
