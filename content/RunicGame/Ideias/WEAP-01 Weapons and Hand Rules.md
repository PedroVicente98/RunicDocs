**ID:** WEAP-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Weapon Class System#Loadouts|Weapon Class System › Loadouts]]

Each slot gives fixed basic actions; weapon skills are the basic kit, unlocked by level, and [[Weapon Class System#Disciplines|Discipline]] skills are the main kit ([[DISC-03 Disciplines as Skill Pools|DISC-03]]). Main hand: light and heavy attack. Off hand: secondary action (block, parry, channel). 2H: light, heavy and its own action.

There are no versatile weapons: each weapon is either one-handed or two-handed, and the only pairs are the ten [[Weapon Class System#Loadouts|loadouts]] ([[CLASS-02 Weapons and Named Classes|CLASS-02]]).

No weapon swap in combat; the loadout changes only at the [[Real exploration#Altars, death, respawn and teleports|Altar]] reset.

Launch loadouts: Sword \+ Shield, Wand \+ Shield, Wand \+ Focus, Spear, Quarterstaff, Staff, Bow, Instrument (Lute, Harp, Flute, Drum and others, two-handed), Greatsword and Dual Axes. Each has a signature mechanic (Spear stagger meter, Sword riposte, Greatsword [[Defense Evasion Guard and armor#Guard|Guard]] break, Quarterstaff flow, Bow charged shots, Instrument resonance; the Dual Axes' is to define). There is no ranged slot: melee weapons have a few long-range skills at the root of their tree ([[EQUIP-01 Equipment Slots|EQUIP-01]]). Expansion candidates: hand crossbow, harpoon, gauntlets.

*Updated 2026-10-30: ten fixed loadouts (was: versatile weapons with 1H and 2H grips and grip skills; swapping inside the Vertente; a catalog with Dagger, Axe, Mace, War Axe and hand instruments, with Axe bleed, Mace concussion and Dagger backstab and poison).*
