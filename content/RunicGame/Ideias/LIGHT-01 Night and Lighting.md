**ID:** LIGHT-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Real exploration#Night and lighting|Real exploration › Night and lighting]]

Night is really dark: without light you barely see the terrain and the enemies.

Torch: takes the off hand and lights well, so it trades a weapon for light. Belt lantern: doesn’t take the hands, uses oil and lights less. Skills with the Illumination component: light radius, duration and intensity, like any component, so passives, item effects and interactions can read it (ex.: light-sensitive creatures back off).

Light has a price: some creatures are attracted by it, and in PvP it reveals your position. Light exists on the server as data, not only in rendering. The same system works in the Underground.
