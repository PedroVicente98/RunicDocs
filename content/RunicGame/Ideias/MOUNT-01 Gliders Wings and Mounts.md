**ID:** MOUNT-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Movement and vehicles|Movement and vehicles]]

Glider (ArcheAge style): only glides. It is the first way to glide and uses the wind currents of the regions with that gimmick.

Wing: equipment, equipped and not summoned. It glides like the glider and flies in the flight zones.

Mounts with a function: glide and fly are separate capabilities, and a mount can have one, both or none. There are also mounts that swim and that climb. Function is what makes a mount desirable, not just speed.

The other vehicles are summoned by scroll. Flying vehicles work only in the flight zones. Ships of several sizes are essential for [[Trade packs and routes|trade packs]], housing islands and naval raids.
