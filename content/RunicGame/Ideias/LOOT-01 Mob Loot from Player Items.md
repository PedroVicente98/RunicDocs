**ID:** LOOT-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Economy housing and land|Economy, housing and land › The world economy]] (black market)

Mobs can drop itens sold by players, like Albion that has black market that put sold items on loot poll. I think it should be integrated into the auction house. So any item can be sold at the auction house section, and mobs of the associated item level being dropping the itens.

Have a table in database that keeps the drops added by players, it would need the items id, the player id, the rarity, the level, value, etc. So mobs at level 20 drop itens at that scale. Now is the price you gain better or worse than normal sell? i think it should be better the the average but worse than top auction. Also do i limit to equipments only? or resources too?

Need some balancing overview. But the main ideia is that mobs can drop items made by players.

Decided 2026-10-30 (black market): four passive goblins, one in an alley of each faction capital, one in the neutral continent's starting city and one on the pirate island. They buy only crafted equipment, which carries its crafter's name, at the current average auction price; each piece sold lowers their price for that type and tier until mobs drop the stock. Bought items go into the drops of mobs of that tier, and a piece sold to them a second time is destroyed, like any vendor sale. Mobs never drop gold, only materials and coin purses that cost [[Labor|labor]] to open. Details: [[Nodes Economy and Exploration|RunicGame — Appendix: Nodes, Economy and Exploration]].
