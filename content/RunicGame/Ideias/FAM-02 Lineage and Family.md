**ID:** FAM-02 · **Status:** Partial · **Game vision:** [[Real exploration#Maps as a resource|Real exploration › Maps as a resource]]; [[Real exploration#Professions|Real exploration › Professions]] ([[Labor|labor]]); [[Economy housing and land|Economy, housing and land]]

The account level is now called Lineage (Family in [[FAM-01 Account and Alts and The Family|FAM-01]] and in earlier entries). Everything account-wide belongs to it: labor, map discovery, the land limit, the wardrobe and the shared storage. The Lineage sees on the map every [[Real exploration#Altars, death, respawn and teleports|Altar]] its characters found, but respawn and teleports use only each character's own Altars ([[ALTAR-01 Altars Death and Respawn|ALTAR-01]]).

**Family** is a mini-group per character, like ArcheAge's, with its own chat and shared permissions. Families can mix factions. A character can be in only one family, besides a marriage.

**Permissions** are per character. Characters of the same Lineage use everything from each other; things from other players are used only by the character that received the permission. This stops chains of shared permissions through several marriages.

**Not in [[Vision|the game vision]] yet:** the Family mini-group, its chat and its permissions.
