**ID:** SITE-01 · **Status:** Open

Fragments are a Cartography consumable that never runs out. The flow: find fragments, combine them, decipher them, travel, open the site.

1. **Find.** Every fragment has a tier, a theme and the region where it dropped. Sources: mobs (tier by mob level, like coin purses), coin purses (higher purse tiers drop higher fragments), fishing (a bottle with a map, wreck theme), ruins and summits (exploration finds) and rewards from other sites (one tier up). Themes: Wreck (at sea, you dive into it), Cache (on land, you dig it up), Ruin (you open a sealed door), Lair (a rare creature's den, tied to [[BEST-01 Bestiary|BEST-01]]) and Rift (a Chaos dungeon, [[RIFT-01 Chaos Rifts|RIFT-01]]).  
2. **Combine.** Tier 1 needs 1 fragment, tier 2 needs 2, tier 3 needs 3 and tier 4 needs 4 plus special paper. All of them share tier, theme and region; the source doesn't matter. Fragments are tradeable.  
3. **Decipher.** At the cartographer's table, for Cartography [[Labor|labor]], paper and ink. Proficiency gates the tiers, and tiers 3 and 4 need the mastery. The minigame aligns the landmarks sketched on the fragments (a coastline, peaks, a ruin's outline) with the region's terrain. Quality sets precision, never success: there is no failure. Batch mode skips the minigame and gives the widest search area.  
4. **Site map.** A unique item: tradeable, never printable, and it doesn't expire. It shows the node, the landmark sketch and a search circle (ex.: 10 m deciphered perfectly, 60 m in batch mode). *(proposal: one active site map per character.)*  
5. **Site.** Chosen when deciphering, from site volumes placed by designers in each region, and reserved while the map is active. It spawns when the map holder gets close, in living-world style ([[LIVE-01 Living World|LIVE-01]]). To activate it, you dig, dive or break the seal, and guardians appear, scaled to the tier.  
6. **Risk.** The node decides it. In peaceful nodes the site is PvE. In conflict zones it is PvPvE, and once activated it gives off a visible signal (smoke, a glow) to PvP players nearby. Tier 3 and 4 sites roll more often in dangerous nodes.  
7. **Rewards.** Materials, including non-craftable ones (one of the 2+ sources in [[PRICE-01 Prices and Material Sources|PRICE-01]]), coin, lore manuscripts for Printing's books, a rare creature at Lair sites, sometimes a higher-tier fragment, and cosmetics.

On the job board, deciphering fragments is a contract type, with the fragments held in escrow ([[JOB-01 Job Board|JOB-01]]).
