**ID:** VIS-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Business model launch visuals and audio#Visuals|Business model, launch, visuals and audio › Visuals]]

The visual side and collecting mounts and pets, like in WoW, should be interesting. Transmog is an account-wide wardrobe applied as a visual slot: getting an item unlocks its look forever. The idea of visuals and pets is interesting. Special mounts with specific functions. Mounts that glide could be nice, so the glider isn't needed, and flying mounts work only in the flight zones.

*Updated 2026-10-16: transmog is an account-wide wardrobe applied as a visual slot (was: transmog or visual slot, to decide); flying mounts in flight zones (was: flight will never be added).*
