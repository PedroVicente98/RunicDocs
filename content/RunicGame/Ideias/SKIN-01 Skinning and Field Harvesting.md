**ID:** SKIN-01 · **Status:** Open

Killing a monster gives its standard drop, but the body can also be worked: you interact with it and skin it with a knife to get leather, hide and other parts. I saw this in ArcheAge Chronicles. The most basic creatures can be skinned by anyone, with no profession level; others need a minimum profession level, so players go out hunting in the field for specific hides.

The idea is interactivity with the world: interacting with animal bodies, with living animals, with trees, and so on.

**Open:** which profession owns it, an existing one or a new one. *(proposal: Husbandry, which in ArcheAge already butchers livestock, so everything about animals stays in one profession: raising, butchering and skinning; Leatherwork stays the crafting side.)*

*(proposal: skinning follows the profession rules ([[PROF-02 Professions and Masteries|PROF-02]]): it spends a little [[Labor|labor]]; the knife goes in the profession tool slot and its quality raises the yield; a short interaction with an optional timing step raises quality, and batch mode gives the plain result. The body stays skinnable for a short time, only for whoever has loot rights, and the character stands still while skinning, which is a risk in PvP areas. How the creature died can change the hide: a burned pelt loses quality. The bestiary ([[BEST-01 Bestiary|BEST-01]]) shows what each species yields once it is studied, and rare hides can be one of the 2+ sources of a material ([[PRICE-01 Prices and Material Sources|PRICE-01]]).)*
