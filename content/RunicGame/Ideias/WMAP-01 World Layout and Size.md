**ID:** WMAP-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Open world|Open world]]

The main world is one continuous map: the surface, with at least three continents separated by seas full of islands, the floating islands above it and the Underground below it. The Sky Island is a separate map, the same size as the surface. Other maps connect by portal.

To start, each map is about 600 km², counting land, islands and sea; new regions come with the live game.

Inspiration: Tales of Demons and Gods (without separate dimensions as a theme) and the exploration and nature feeling of Crimson Desert. Everything is interactable: ruins, magical places, floating islands, desolate places and hard mobs.
