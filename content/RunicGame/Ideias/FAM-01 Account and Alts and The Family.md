**ID:** FAM-01 · **Status:** Partial · **Game vision:** [[Economy housing and land|Economy, housing and land]] (limit per account); [[Real exploration#Maps as a resource|Real exploration › Maps as a resource]] (map discovery per Lineage); [[Business model launch visuals and audio#Visuals|Visuals]] (account wardrobe)

Account characters share Famility name.  
Need to be alt friendly, not multi account friendly.  
Achievements and Completionism is account wide, so all character share same progress that can be account wide, like achievements, map discovery, other challenges, etc.  
Banks and Storage are shared, also land ownership is family based, so a player with 1 character and another with 10 have the same number of lands.  
The Family has shared inventory in the characters for specific itens, like scriptures, custom currencies, money, etc.  
[[Labor|Labor]] is also family shared.

**Not in [[Vision|the game vision]] yet:** family name; alt friendly, not multi account friendly; account-wide achievements and challenges; shared bank and storage; the Lineage shared inventory (scriptures, custom currencies, money).

*Updated 2026-10-30: family-shared labor and account-wide map discovery are in the game vision, under the name Lineage ([[FAM-02 Lineage and Family|FAM-02]]); respawn and teleports use only the [[Real exploration#Altars, death, respawn and teleports|Altars]] each character has discovered, so every character has to rediscover them ([[ALTAR-01 Altars Death and Respawn|ALTAR-01]]).*
