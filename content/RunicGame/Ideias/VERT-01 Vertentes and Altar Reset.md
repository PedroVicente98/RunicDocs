**ID:** VERT-01 · **Status:** Discarded · **Game vision:** [[Archived and discarded|Recorded decisions and next steps › Archived and discarded]]

A Vertente unlocks a group of weapons. Using weapons from another Vertente, or changing [[Weapon Class System#Disciplines|Discipline]], needs a full reset at an [[Real exploration#Altars, death, respawn and teleports|Altar]] (every city has one), paid with in-game gold; the first one can be free. The reset also redistributes skill points.

Four Vertentes as the base, adjusted during development: Marcial (Sword, Axe, Mace, Spear, Shield, Greatsword, War Axe), Ágil (Sword, Dagger, Axe, Bow), Arcana (Wand, Arcane Focus, Staff, Instruments, Shield), Mística (Quarterstaff, Mace, Shield, Arcane Focus).

The Vertente doesn’t restrict the Discipline. Why limit: stronger identity, readability in PvP (the enemy knows which weapon family to expect) and focus on the weapon trees.

*Updated 2026-10-30: discarded on 2026-10-25. The class is one of ten fixed [[Weapon Class System#Loadouts|loadouts]] plus a Discipline, both chosen at the Altar ([[CLASS-02 Weapons and Named Classes|CLASS-02]]). The Altar reset stays: changing the loadout or the Discipline needs a full reset at an Altar, paid with in-game gold (the first one can be free), and it redistributes the skill points.*
