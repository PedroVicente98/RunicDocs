**ID:** ART-01 · **Status:** Open

There is no instrument slot. Instrument is a tag: item instruments (flute, guitar), weapon instruments (Lute, Harp, Flute, Drum) and stations (pianos and others) can all play. Using a sheet opens a list of every instrument equipped and in the inventory; at a station, the station is the only option. Sheets work on any instrument.

Artistry is playing and composing. Composing uses an in-game editor almost 1:1 with ArcheAge's (MML text). Proficiency lowers mistakes from easy to hard sheets. A beginner composing complex music makes typos and missing notes; at mastery you play and compose anything with no mistakes. Music paper is a Printing special paper.

**Open:** the pure ArcheAge model (proficiency sets a character limit on the music paper, and a song can't be played below the writer's proficiency), this error model, or a mix of both.

Performance is roleplay only, with no buffs; buffs come only from weapons. You can play in combat. Hard CC (stun, stagger, trip, knockback) or moving cancels it; slows and roots don't. Playing on a sinking ship until death is the player's choice.

*(proposal, network: one start event with who, sheet, instrument, world start time and a random seed, about 30 to 50 bytes; clients synthesize the notes locally; a sheet is a few KB, cached by its hash; the seed makes every listener hear the same mistakes; ensembles sync from the world time.)*
