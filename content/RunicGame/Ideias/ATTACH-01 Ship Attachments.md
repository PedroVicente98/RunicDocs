**ID:** ATTACH-01 · **Status:** Open

Things that stick to ships. An attachment is a small entity that latches onto a ship's hull. It has health, and its effect is data: debuffs, damage, tracking, attacking the players aboard and more. Examples:

* **Hull Leech:** drains hull points like a breach that can't be patched, only killed.  
* **Drowned Chains:** extra weight, so the ship sits lower and moves slower.  
* **Brine Eye:** tracking: it reveals the ship's position to monsters or to whoever sent it.  
* **Spawning Sac:** spawns creatures that board the ship and attack the crew.  
* **Mourning Coral:** the crew on deck takes Madness build-up.  
* **Lantern Eater:** puts out the ship's lights, so night and fog get darker.

**Sources:** each sea region has its own type of attachment, so no region is 100% safe, though some have only the most basic ones. They come from sea monsters, ghost-ship events, Chaos rifts and cursed waters.

**Removal:** some are hit from the deck, some need someone leaning over the side, at risk of falling, and some die to fire or to Support cleanses. It's a crew job, like bailing, and gives melee players a role at sea. Each ship takes 1 to 8 attachments, by ship type: the most basic ship takes 1, a big galleon 8\. *(proposal: some fall off in fresh water or sunlight.)*
