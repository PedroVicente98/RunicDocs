**ID:** BIZ-01 · **Status:** Being designed · **Game vision:** [[Business model launch visuals and audio#Business model|Business model, launch, visuals and audio › Business model]]

A subscription, and no cash shop under any circumstance, not even for cosmetics, are decided. So the [[Inventory equipment and the Market Isle#Market Isle (working name)|Market Isle]] ([[MARKET-01 Market Isle|MARKET-01]]) never sells anything for real money ([[SUB-01 Subscription|SUB-01]]).

**Open:** the base model next to the [[Business model launch visuals and audio#Subscription|subscription]]: free-to-play (limited free play, with the subscription giving full access) or buy-to-play. One possibility is free-to-play plus the subscription, with buy-to-play expansions, but it isn't decided ([[LAUNCH-01 Launch|LAUNCH-01]]).
