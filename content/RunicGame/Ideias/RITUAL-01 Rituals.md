**ID:** RITUAL-01 · **Status:** Open

Most things players do together are rituals: an item that only the controller uses, while the other players join by standing in the circle and channeling. The divine level of a ritual is the divine item ([[GOD-01 Divine Items|GOD-01]]). Group skills cast together (unison) were considered and dropped: each character's combos between their own skills are enough.

* **Controller:** the player who uses the item controls the effect. Removing the controller cancels the cast completely.  
* **Circle:** removing people from the circle lowers the effect or lengthens the cast, depending on the ritual.  
* **Interruption:** damage to a participant's health takes them out of the animation. Shields prevent it, and so do skills that redirect damage to the Tank.  
* **Cast time:** depends on the effect: most rituals take 5 to 15 s, or longer. A divine ritual needs 5 people and 30 s to 1 min of cast, with a very big animation.  
* **Where:** sieges, ship battles and raids (ex.: the storm ritual, [[SHIP-01 Naval Combat Flooding Repair and Sea Events|SHIP-01]]). It's a tactical element for any group activity, better or worse depending on the situation: without a Tank to defend the cast, a ritual is easy to cancel and usually not worth it.  
* **Roleplay rituals** also exist, and the idea of items used together can be reused across the game.  
* **Band ([[ART-01 Instruments Performance and Artistry|ART-01]]):** simpler: when players play together, the music just syncs, with no gameplay effect. Tying rituals to instruments needs more thought.

**Open:** whether condition ticks (Burn, Bleed, Poison) also take a participant out, since they damage health; and the Tank's Challenge only redirects part of the damage, so on its own it wouldn't protect a participant. *(proposal: condition ticks count, so Support cleanses become part of defending a ritual; Defense has a skill that takes all the damage of one participant for a few seconds; runes on the ground mark the circle's spots; long-lasting ritual zones are objects with health that enemies can destroy, with a limit per region; on the server a ritual is one entity with a participant list, about 10 bytes to start and 6 bytes per join or leave.)*
