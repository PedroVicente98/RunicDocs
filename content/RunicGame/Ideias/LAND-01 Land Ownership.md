**ID:** LAND-01 · **Status:** Partial · **Game vision:** [[Economy housing and land|Economy, housing and land]]

Big problem in archeage was monopoly of land, and unusable space due to poor land placement, so housing zones should be segmented in 8x8 slots, so the small, medium and large lands, being 8x8, 16x16 and 32x32, would always occupy the fixed number of slots.

Also limit the number of land ownership per player(not character), either globally or regionally(continent 1, continent 2, open sea islands, wild continent, sky islands, underground regions). So there is no big monopoly and allow everyone a chance.

Allow for a tax fund, where weekly tax is auto paid using this fund, so if tax is 15g per week you put 150g then you get 10 weeks of auto pay tax. This is so players dont feel the need to enter just to pay taxes. Also, when a land is not paid taxes,, the player never loses anything, the plot is freed to other players, but the contents are saved and the owner receive the scripture with all the things in the plot, farm itens keep their status, so its a freeze. When putting the land back, in another spot it checks the region, if there are plants and farm itens that are specific to a different region, it auto collects if ready or removes and return a seed if not, so the player has a net neutral. This allow for a changin landscape of plot owners and players dont lose the things they spent time working towards. Also moving the plot with all its contents is possible, the same ideia, but manually started but removing the plot and getting the scripture to put in another place. When player can only pay taxes and place houses if they have active subscriptions, so if they stop playing they just are stuck with the scripture until they pay again, also dont lose progress.

Land is shared between characters, the scriptures goes in a shared inventory fo the family.

**Not in [[Vision|the game vision]] yet:** scriptures (Deeds) in the Lineage shared inventory (see [[FAM-01 Account and Alts and The Family|FAM-01]]).
