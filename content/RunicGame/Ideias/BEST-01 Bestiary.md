**ID:** BEST-01 · **Status:** Open

The bestiary isn't a profession. Knowledge about each species grows in stages, like Terraria's bestiary: seen, killed several times, then studied with observation minigames that use tools (ex.: a spyglass). The stages reveal appearance and habitat, then behavior and drops, then creature traits and weaknesses ([[DMG-01 Damage Types|DMG-01]]).

Studying costs no [[Labor|labor]], because the bestiary isn't a profession. The labor is spent when a player with the Printing mastery prints an entry on bestiary paper, to sell the mob information. *(proposal: reading a printed entry unlocks it for the Lineage and consumes the page.)*

Cartography only maps where mobs spawn (the fauna layer). That is one subsection of information, not the bestiary.

*(proposal: an entry gives an in-game benefit, ex.: a weakness hint when you hit that species, so a fan wiki gives the information but not the benefit.)*
