**ID:** SND-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Business model launch visuals and audio#Custom soundtrack (client only)|Business model, launch, visuals and audio › Custom soundtrack]] (client only)

In the ClientSide, i want to make it possible for the player to load any music file the player wants. So i map out all music by context (combat, farming, sailing, city), and the music can be changed by the player directly in the UI, with albums and presets per context, it should be an advanced functionality to those that want a custom soundtrack. Sound effects, like hit sounds and combat cues, can’t be changed, so every player hears the same combat warnings. Can even be something radio like for those that dont care about combat music in combat, do a combat radio in combat, fam radio while farming, etc. the radio would be multiple tracks played, save progress and last track and play next.

This could be all client side, dont have any effect aside from the user experience.

*Updated 2026-10-16: only music can be changed; sound effects are locked (was: every single audio event, background music or hit sounds, can be changed).*
