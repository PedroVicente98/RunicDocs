**ID:** CMB-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Combat|Combat]]; [[Defense Evasion Guard and armor|Defense: Evasion, Guard and armor]]; [[Skills by components|Skills by components]]

Combat is action with specific targeting skills.

It will be a mix of Black Desert key combo, with Archeage Style type of skill combo(one skill set up special effects with other skills are used), with Tera, where there is a combo key(space) to auto chain skills. Still figuring out how it would look like, and how it would be implemented, it will implement a action and target system and improve and tweak from there until its a good and complex enough.

Slower combat more tactical and positional, with longer skills cooldown, with more significant skills, multiple weapon types for different playstyles, 1H Sword and Shild, 2H Sword, Spears, Axes, Bows, Staffs.

Each weapon come with skills for light, heavy and offhand(block or other skill)

skills can have synergy with the weapon.

Weapons can have different effects, spears might have a stagger meter on enemies, Dual Axes to define, staffs magic or concussion, etc.

*Updated 2026-10-30: Bleed belongs to the War [[Weapon Class System#Disciplines|Discipline]], and the Dual Axes' signature mechanic is to define (was: Axes bleeds).*
