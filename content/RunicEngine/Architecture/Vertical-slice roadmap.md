Each slice is small, ends in something runnable and testable, and adds one piece of this architecture on top of the previous ones. The Packages column names the work packages each slice needs (defined under Modules, work packages and the implementation contract); a slice is done when those packages are merged and its runnable result passes. Slices with a letter (20a, 23a, 25a, 33a, 42a, 42b, 43a) were inserted later between their neighbors, so existing numbers never change. Godot is part of the build from slice [[Slice 00 Build skeleton|0]]: it may stay idle or headless while the simulation is built, and each slice pulls one piece of presentation or tooling into it (the Godot column).
