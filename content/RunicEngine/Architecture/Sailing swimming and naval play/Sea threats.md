* Each sea region has its own creatures (sharks, reef creatures, sea monsters) using CharacterWaterMovement, [[Behavior-tree runtime|behavior trees]] and perception profiles; some are drawn to ship lanterns at night.

* Small boats are more vulnerable: creatures can target hulls as well as swimmers.

* Water bosses use SwimMovementModifiers for charges, dives and breaches.
