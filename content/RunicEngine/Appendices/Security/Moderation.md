* **Reports carry evidence:** the last 60 seconds (example) of facts and positions around the reported player, attached automatically ([[GSRV-9|GSRV-9]]).
* **Reports are weighted** by the reporter's history, so mass false reports do nothing ([[GSVC-12|GSVC-12]]).
* **Sanction ladder:** warning, mute, trade lock, temporary ban, permanent ban; bots and real-money trading go straight to a ban ([[GSVC-12|GSVC-12]]). Mutes, trade locks and kicks act in the simulation at once ([[GSRV-9|GSRV-9]]).
* **Appeals**, and an audit log of every GM action ([[GSVC-12|GSVC-12]], [[PSDK-1|PSDK-1]]).
* **Chat:** rate limits, size limits and spam filtering ([[PCS-5|PCS-5]]).
