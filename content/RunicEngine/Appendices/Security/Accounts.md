Most stolen accounts come from reused passwords (credential stuffing), phishing pages, and fake launchers or cheat downloads that steal passwords.

* **Platform ([[PCS-1|PCS-1]]):** login rate limits per address and per account; Argon2id password hashing; a known-breach check on new passwords; 2FA with authenticator apps and passkeys; new-device detection with an email; sign-out everywhere.
* **Game ([[GSVC-4|GSVC-4]]):** after a login from a new device, trades, mail and auction withdrawals wait (example 24 hours); young accounts have transfer limits (example: their first 7 days).
* **Signed launcher**, so a fake launcher cannot pass as the real one ([[GAME-3|GAME-3]]).
