Hands-on lab for the two target slots of action combat: the action target picked from the crosshair, and the Ward that target skills go to (MMB tap and hold, wheel cycle, the no-Ward fallback), plus placed and target-centered areas and dual-use skills. Every setting can be changed on the page. The rules come from the combat review in progress (November 2026); [[Combat]] gets them once they are decided.
