Working prototype of the knowledge system behind NPC conversations and the knowledge tab. Entries are tagged by facet and by depth (Common, Doctrine, Expertise, Hidden, Lost); NPC profiles say what each NPC knows and how much trust each depth needs; the simulator shows what an NPC would tell a given character, and why; and campaign notes turn into entries through knowledge callouts. The design is still in progress (November 2026); [[RunicGame — Quest Design]] gets it once it is decided.

The page runs in your browser and saves there. It ships empty, because the lore stays off this public site: bring data in with Import, from an export of another copy.
