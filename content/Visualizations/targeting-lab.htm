Targeting Lab

Combat is pure action: hostile skills go where you aim. Two slots only carry information and friendly targets. The action target is picked from the crosshair and shows its frame; the Ward is the ally (or you) that target skills go to. Try the rules and their settings on a small skirmish seen from above, where the cursor stands in for the crosshair.
