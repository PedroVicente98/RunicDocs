**ID:** VAL-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Combat]] (PvE and PvP values)

Every value (damage, percentages, durations, cooldowns) has a base, and the skill, item or effect can declare its own PvE and/or PvP values. Ex.: base 100, PvP 85, PvE 105\. *(Proposal: the context is decided by the target of each hit.)*
