**ID:** LOCK-01 · **Status:** Open

* **Chaos rifts:** no limit.  
* **Raids:** loot 3 times a week; the value will be tuned.  
* **World bosses:** loot once a week. After that you can still fight and deal damage, with no chance of loot.

Limits are weekly, never daily (pillar 8). WoW applies lockouts per character, which makes alts feel necessary. *(proposal: lockouts per Lineage, so alts are never an obligation.)*
