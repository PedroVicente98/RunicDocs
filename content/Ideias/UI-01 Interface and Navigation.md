**ID:** UI-01 · **Status:** Open

* **Minimal UI** with good menu design, inspired by Elder Scrolls Online's.  
* **No minimap,** only a compass.  
* **Toggleable and movable:** the whole UI can be hidden, and every panel can be hidden and moved on its own, inspired by Final Fantasy XIV's. No addons.  
* **Auto-pathing** only on roads and in mapped areas, inspired by Black Desert's.

*(proposal: the compass shows directions plus only the markers the player has unlocked, from quests or chart layers; a mapped area is one where the player has the node's base chart, [[MAP-01 Maps as a Resource|MAP-01]].)*
