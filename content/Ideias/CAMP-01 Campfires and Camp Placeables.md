**ID:** CAMP-01 · **Status:** Open · **Related:** [[SOC-01 Emotes and Marriage|SOC-01]], [[ART-01 Instruments, Performance and Artistry|ART-01]], [[LIGHT-01 Night and Lighting|LIGHT-01]], [[Factions, PvP and crime#Jurisdiction|Jurisdiction]]

Campfires and other placeable things that buff the players around them and create a social moment. There is downtime, and during downtime the campfire gives the social possibilities.

It can be other things too: a tent; at sea, a buoy or a raft to relax on; grills to cook food. Players can set up a place to meet people in the wilderness. It has to be in a safe space, or they can be attacked.

**Open:**

* What the buffs are, how long they last after leaving, and whether more people around make them stronger.
* Which placeables exist (campfire, tent, grill, raft, buoy, others), how they are made (crafted, cooked, bought) and how long they last before they go out or break.
* Where they can be placed: anywhere, only outside towns and housing plots, or only in safe spaces.
* Whether a camp in the open world can be attacked, by players and by mobs, or whether something keeps a camp safe.
* How they connect to other systems: cooking at the grill, music at the fire (Artistry), firelight at night, warmth in cold weather, and rafts and buoys drifting with the current.
