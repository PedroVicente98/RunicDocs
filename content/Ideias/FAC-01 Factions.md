**ID:** FAC-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Factions, PvP and crime]]

There should be 3 factions. Faction Continent 1, Faction Continent 2 and Pirate. The player creates a player starting in one of the continent factions, during gameplay the player can take actions to improve faction standing, change factions to continent faction 2 OR Pirate Factions.
