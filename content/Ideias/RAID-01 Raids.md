**ID:** RAID-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[PvE]]

In ArcheAge the focus was very much on PvP; there weren't many dungeons, and raids didn't even exist.

I think it would be good to have more engaging PvE content. Raids have exclusive materials, and each dungeon and raid has exclusive things, if not for power upgrades then possibly exclusive cosmetics. The materials can be used together with drops to craft the endgame weapons used in PvP.
