**ID:** PVX-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[How the systems connect]]; [[Factions, PvP and crime]]

PvP \- \> Gold, Honor, Special Resources, etc  
PvE \-\> Mats and Equipments  
Open World \-\> Mats and Gold (via Housing), Regional Resources  
Trade Pack \-\> Gold, Special Resources and PvP

Pvp is Faction vs Faction with Crime PK being Same Faction PK. enough crime change someone to a pirate. Some Crime PK is also needed to change continent factions.

PvE is dungeons, raids, world bosses, or mob farms, special mobs in open world that are rare. PvE can only be made with people of your faction; pirates can do PvE with both continent factions.

*Updated 2026-10-16: pirates can do PvE with both continent factions (was: PvE only with people of your faction).*
