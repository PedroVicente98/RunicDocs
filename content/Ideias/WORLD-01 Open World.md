**ID:** WORLD-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Open world]]

The Map needs to be Gigantic, with many regions.  
3 continents at least  
1 continent for each faction  
1+ neutral continents  
Between Continents needs large seas with many islands.  
Each Region can have a gimmick, to make the exploration interesting.  
Exploration and Navigating the World is the main focus.
