**ID:** LORE-03 · **Status:** Open

The player is a mercenary of the faction: loyal to it, but not a soldier, acting like the adventurers' guilds in fantasy stories. Factions have NPC soldiers, and a player may earn titles like soldier or commander through quests.

With the Anchored ([[LORE-02 Why Adventurers Come Back|LORE-02]]), factions hire adventurers because they come back from death, but they don't fit the military chain of command. **Open:** a trace of divinity that explains why adventurers are stronger.
