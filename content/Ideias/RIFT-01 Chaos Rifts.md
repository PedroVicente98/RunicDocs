**ID:** RIFT-01 · **Status:** Open

Random dungeons opened by the god of Chaos ([[LORE-01 Pantheon|LORE-01]]). The game keeps opening invisible spatial rifts at random points, anywhere: underwater, on a mountain top. You see one only by passing right next to it or with a map: lore scrolls deciphered with Cartography reveal where a rift is ([[SITE-01 Map Fragments and Sites|SITE-01]], Rift theme).

Modular and close to a roguelike: a library of rooms with specific mechanics, in different orders, and some rifts have rooms that others don't. The theme comes from a base monster (ex.: a wolf): the mobs are based on it, not only that monster.

The level stays near the area's. An area of level 25 usually gets a rift of 25 to 35; higher levels are rarer, with a decreasing chance (ex.: 25–30 70%, 31–35 24%, 36–45 5%, 46–50 1%).

Rewards are exclusive items, and rifts are another source of materials that also come from elsewhere ([[PRICE-01 Prices and Material Sources|PRICE-01]]). Rifts can be done without limit ([[LOCK-01 Loot Lockouts|LOCK-01]]).

*(proposal: rooms with connectors, and the theme swaps the cast, materials and boss, so 30 rooms × 10 themes reuse each room 10 times; instanced per group; a rift lasts about 24 hours or until cleared; supply is tuned by how often each theme spawns, not by the drop rate.)*
