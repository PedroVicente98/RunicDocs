**ID:** FLOAT-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Open world]]

Floating islands are inside the surface map (ex.: 1,000 m up), fixed and indestructible; at most they move up and down a little. They hold exploration, resources and magical places. A floating island is never split between two simulation servers.

Floating islands are all in the flight-zone region of the neutral continent: where there are floating islands there is flight. For now that is a single region; more may come later.

*Updated 2026-11-03: floating islands sit in the neutral continent's flight-zone region (was: no set place in the surface map).*
