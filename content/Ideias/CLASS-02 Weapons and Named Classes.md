**ID:** CLASS-02 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Weapon Class System]]

Locked on 2026-10-25. Ten fixed loadouts and five Disciplines ([[DISC-04 Five Disciplines|DISC-04]]) make 50 named classes.

* **Loadouts:** Sword \+ Shield, Wand \+ Shield, Wand \+ Focus, Spear, Quarterstaff, Staff, Bow, Instrument, Greatsword and Dual Axes.  
* **Removed:** Mace, Dagger, War Axe, the one-handed Axe as its own loadout and hand instruments as off hands. Axes exist only as Dual Axes, and the Instrument is two-handed.  
* **No versatile weapons:** each weapon is either one-handed or two-handed, and the only pairs are the loadouts above.  
* **Choosing a class:** at the Altar, the player picks the loadout and the Discipline, which locks the named class. Changing either needs the Altar reset, and there is no weapon-type swap outside it. Vertentes no longer exist ([[VERT-01 Vertentes and Altar Reset|VERT-01]]).  
* **Roles:** Damage, Tank and Support (heals and buffs), in similar numbers: 18 Damage, 15 Tank and 17 Support. War is always Damage, Defense is always Tank (Support on the Bow) and Vitality is always Support; Arcane and Occultism change by loadout.  
* **English names:** every specific name in the game is in English, the first localization, since the game is global.

Class names, in the order War · Defense · Vitality · Arcane · Occultism, with the role shown where it varies:

* **Sword \+ Shield:** Legionnaire · Guardian · Paladin · Rune Knight (Tank) · Blood Knight (Tank)  
* **Wand \+ Shield:** Battlemage · Spellwarden · Chaplain · Runeguard (Tank) · Pactbinder (Support)  
* **Wand \+ Focus:** Warcaster · Shieldweaver · Priest · Arcanist (Damage) · Warlock (Support)  
* **Spear:** Lancer · Phalanx · Valkyrie · Stormlancer (Damage) · Impaler (Tank)  
* **Quarterstaff:** Monk · Forest Guardian · Druid · Earthshaper (Tank) · Bog Witch (Support)  
* **Staff:** Devastator · Veilwarden · Oracle · Archmage (Damage) · Necromancer (Damage)  
* **Bow:** Ranger · Vigilant (Support) · Sacred Archer · Arcane Archer (Damage) · Plaguebow (Support)  
* **Instrument:** Skald · Songshield · Bard · Stormsinger (Damage) · Enchanter (Support)  
* **Greatsword:** Champion · Sentinel · Crusader · Spellblade (Damage) · Revenant (Tank)  
* **Dual Axes:** Berserker · Juggernaut · Shaman · Flamereaver (Damage) · Blood Shaman (Support)

**Katana:** not added; its animation style is too different from the Greatsword's.
