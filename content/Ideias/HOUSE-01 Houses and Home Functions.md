**ID:** HOUSE-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Economy, housing and land]] › Houses

* Houses live only on open-world plots.
* Designs are two-floor models with balconies and covered space under them.
* The house is a workshop and a farm, not a shop or a bank:
  * farming;
  * recall with a cost and a cooldown;
  * some crafting stations, which need the profession's mastery to buy;
  * the packing bench and the processing station;
  * non-combat buffs from rare furniture;
  * no bank, commerce, workers or expeditions.
* Climate changes crop efficiency, not what grows.

**Proposals kept here (not in the Vision):**

* **Measures for the designs:**
  * 4 m per floor, because the third-person camera needs headroom.
  * Rooms on the 4 m module (4x8 m or 8x8 m).
  * U-shaped stairs of 4x4 m per floor.
  * 36 small crops per 8x8 m, ArcheAge's density.

  These are art and balance numbers, so they go in data tables.

* **Models by plot:**

  | Model | Plot | House | Balcony and covered space | Rest of the plot |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Garden annex | 8x8 | No two-floor house: the stairs alone would take half a floor | – | About 36 small crops |
  | Balcony house | 16x16 | 8x12 m, two floors, about 2 rooms and the stairs per floor | 4x8 m balcony over a 32 m² porch | 8x16 m strip, about 72 small crops |
  | Farmhouse | 32x32 | 12x16 m, about 6 rooms per floor | 4x16 m balcony along the house over a 64 m² porch | 32x16 m farm (about 288 small crops) and a 16x16 m yard or pen |
  | Livestock house | 32x32 | 12x16 m in the style of a Minho house (northern Portugal): animals and storage on the ground floor, living upstairs with an outside stair and a veranda | Veranda over the animals' entrance | 32x16 m pasture |
  | Courtyard manor | 32x32 | 28x28 m ring with 8 m wings and a 12x12 m courtyard, about 640 m² per floor | 2 m upper gallery around the courtyard over a covered arcade | Open 8x8 m courtyard (about 36 small crops or an orchard) |

* **The manor and the second plot:** the manor has no farm. That fits the limit of 2 plots per account, since the second plot becomes the farm.
* **The porch:** the packing bench and the processing station go under the balcony, because packs and carts stay at ground level. Crafting stations go in the basement.
* **Why no workers or expeditions:** dispatched production pays without the player being there, and it would compete with carried packs and with real exploration.
