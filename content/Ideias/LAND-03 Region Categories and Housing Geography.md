**ID:** LAND-03 · **Status:** Open · **Related:** [[LAND-01 Land Ownership|LAND-01]], [[LAND-02 Housing Areas and Plot Discovery|LAND-02]], [[CITY-01 Settlements and Neutral Cities|CITY-01]], [[Economy, housing and land]]

**Plot limits:** 2 plots per account. At most one of them can be 32x32, and at most one can be on the neutral continent.

Regions are categorized by their housing: a bigger housing zone, a smaller one, or none. Housing follows the geography: it spreads out from the main cities, and the farther from a city, the more dangerous the land and the scarcer the housing.

**Neutral continent.** This matters most here, because some regions are too hazardous for a housing zone. Some regions are civilized and have a main city, and the big housing zone is around the city. These areas are more urban, with missions related to the story and other things that aren't combat. Outside them housing becomes scarce: a smaller housing zone can sit in another region that isn't as hazardous, and all the other regions are complete wilderness.

**Faction continents.** The same thought, but only a couple of endgame regions have no housing zone.

**Demand.** Not everybody will want a plot on the neutral continent, because it comes with headaches: constant PvP and the expense of teleporting there or going by ship. Most people should be able to get a housing area on the neutral continent or somewhere else in the world.

**Growth.** The world's land can grow to add housing plots, even with regions just for housing (as ArcheAge Classic did). New regions must be planned to make sense with the surrounding regions, and regions with content and housing zones are preferred over housing-only regions.

### Region categories

*(proposal: shares and sizes are examples for data tables.)*

| Category | Where | Housing zone | In a 10 km² region | Plots |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | ---: | ---: |
| City | Neutral continent: a civilized region with a main city, housing around the city | About 30% | 3 km² | About 2,300, mostly 32x32 |
| Frontier | Neutral continent: a less hazardous region near a city region | About 10% | 1 km² | About 770 |
| Wilderness | The rest of the neutral continent | None | – | – |
| Capital or city | Faction continents | About 20% | 2 km² | About 3,200, mixed |
| Standard | Most faction regions and the islands | About 8% | 0.8 km² | About 1,300 |
| Endgame | A couple of regions per faction continent | None | – | – |

1 km² of housing zone holds about 775 plots with the neutral continent's mix (80% 32x32) and about 1,600 with the faction continents' mix, since plots fill about 2/3 of a zone.

### Estimates

*(proposal, 2026-11-03: approximations to plan the map, not final numbers.)*

**Assumptions:** the count is active accounts, since a plot frees up only when its tax lapses. Plots fill 2/3 of a housing zone, and new zones open at 85% occupancy ([[LAND-02 Housing Areas and Plot Discovery|LAND-02]]). About 35% of accounts place a plot on the neutral continent (25% to 50%), 80% of them a 32x32. Most other accounts have one or two plots on the faction continents and islands.

**Demand per 10,000 active accounts:**

| Accounts with a neutral plot | Neutral continent | Faction continents and islands |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| 25% | 2,500 plots, 3.8 km² of housing zone | 11,900 plots, 9.0 km² |
| 35% | 3,500 plots, 5.3 km² | 11,200 plots, 8.3 km² |
| 50% | 5,000 plots, 7.6 km² | 10,300 plots, 7.1 km² |

**Example launch layout** (600 km² of land, regions of about 10 km²):

* Neutral continent, 200 km²: 2 city, 4 frontier and 14 wilderness regions, 10 km² of housing (5% of the continent).
* Each faction continent: 3 capital or city regions, 13 standard and 2 endgame; plus 4 standard island regions. 36 km² of housing in all (9% of their land).
* Whole world: 46 km² of housing, 8% of the land.

**Accounts it serves:** the neutral continent about 19,000 (13,000 to 26,000, by the share that wants a plot there); the faction continents and islands about 44,000 (40,000 to 50,000). When the neutral continent is full, players still find housing on the faction continents, so neutral land is scarce by design.

**Each new region adds room for:** a neutral city region about 5,600 accounts, a frontier region about 1,900, a faction city region about 2,400, a standard region about 1,000 (at 35%).
