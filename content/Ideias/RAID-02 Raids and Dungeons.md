**ID:** RAID-02 · **Status:** Open

* **Raid size:** a flexible mode up to 40 players and a hard mode with 20, like WoW.  
* **No easy mode and no automatic queue:** raid groups form through a premade group finder, like WoW's.  
* **Entry requirements vary by raid:** some need hard dungeons cleared and an achievement line (like attunements in WoW: The Burning Crusade), others a quest line or an item. No single rule applies to every raid.  
* **Solo dungeons:** specific dungeons made for one player (inspired by Vindictus and Dragon Nest), not a solo mode on every dungeon.

**Boss damage:** big damage all the time. Some hits kill outright, tanks included, but every one of them is telegraphed. Cloth wearers die to big hits more often. Each fight is learned by trial and error, like a dance.
