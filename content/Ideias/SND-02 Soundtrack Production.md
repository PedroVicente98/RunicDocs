**ID:** SND-02 · **Status:** Open

A small soundtrack at first. Every region gets a music tag, and tracks repeat across regions. If the game succeeds, new music is added by changing a region's tag to it. It works with the custom soundtrack ([[SND-01 Soundtrack|SND-01]]).

High-end sound effects and voice acting come after successful funding or sales.
