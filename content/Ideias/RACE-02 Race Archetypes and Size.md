**ID:** RACE-02 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Races]]

Five races are decided ([[RACE-01 Races|RACE-01]]): Humans, Elves, Dragonkin, Orcs and the Undead, from [[RunicGame — Lore]] and [[RunicGame — Quest Design]]. Known archetypes are appealing because players already relate to them, and a rich world history can hold several races; other archetypes, like trolls, ogres and giants, can be NPC peoples.

**Size:** every playable race shares one skeleton and one hitbox, so no race is short and PvP has no hitbox advantage. TERA's small races, Popori and Elin, are the usual example of the problem.
