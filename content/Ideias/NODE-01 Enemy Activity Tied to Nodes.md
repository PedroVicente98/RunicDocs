**ID:** NODE-01 · **Status:** Open · **Related:** [[Economy, housing and land]] (prosperity and region projects), [[ECO-01 Economy|ECO-01]], [[TRADE-01 Trade Packs|TRADE-01]], [[LIVE-01 Living World|LIVE-01]], [[MOB-01 Regional and Hard Mobs|MOB-01]], [[CAPRAID-01 Capital Invasion|CAPRAID-01]], [[RIFT-01 Chaos Rifts|RIFT-01]], [[CASTLE-01 Castles|CASTLE-01]]

Enemy activity is tied to the nodes, so prosperity and specific missions can change it. E.g. a mine held by enemies has a liberation mission; once it is done the mine is safe, but players have to keep supplying weapons and resources to keep it safe, and if they stop, the enemies come back.

It fits with the region projects and node prosperity.

*(proposal: each node has a threat level next to its prosperity. The liberation is one of the node's rotating weekly missions. Upkeep is paid with deliveries to the node (weapons, food, repair materials, trade packs), the same kind of deliveries that feed prosperity and region projects. When upkeep lapses, the threat climbs in steps (patrols, then camps, then the enemies retake the site) instead of switching at once, so players see it coming. A site held by enemies lowers or blocks the node's specialty gathering until it is freed again. Enemy movements follow the living world's schedules (LIVE-01).)*

**Open:** whether the guild that holds the node or region is responsible for the upkeep, or anyone can supply it; how fast the threat comes back.
