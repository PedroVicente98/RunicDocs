**ID:** INV-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Inventory, equipment and the Market Isle#Inventory|Inventory, equipment and the Market Isle › Inventory]]

The inventory has no weight, only slots. Carrying a lot never slows the character; the limit is how many items fit.

The character starts with a base number of slots and buys expansions with in-game money *(proposal: each expansion costs more than the last, up to a maximum)*. Trade packs don’t take slots.
