**ID:** DEF-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Defense Evasion, Guard and armor|Defense: Evasion, Guard and armor]]

Everyone defends with two resources: Evasion charges to dodge and Guard to block. Role, Discipline, weapons and armor change how each works, but the resources are the same for all.

Evasion: charges recharge one at a time, and each charge pays a dodge with iframes. The role sets the number of charges (Tank 3, Support 4, Damage 4\) and an effect at the end of the iframe. The Discipline sets the dodge shape (Blink, Shadow Step, Blood Escape…). Special mobility uses the same charge, never a separate pool.

Guard: one resource. Only the shield holds Guard freely and directionally; other weapons have limited Guard and trade defense versatility for damage (Quarterstaff holds it for a limited time, Greatsword during a side dash, Spear while running, magic Guard for mages). That is how a Tank without a shield exists.

Armor: 5 pieces. The iframe duration blends by the armor type of each piece, each piece counting 1/5; Guard is the sum of every piece's Guard stat, which grows with quality; physical and magical armor are separate pools ([[EQUIP-01 Equipment Slots|EQUIP-01]], [[DMG-01 Damage Types|DMG-01]]). *(proposal: cloth has the longest iframe and plate the shortest.)*

*Updated 2026-10-30: armor per piece (was: cloth, leather and plate change the iframe duration, the Guard with a shield and other buffs; proposal: cloth has the longest iframe, plate the most Guard).*
