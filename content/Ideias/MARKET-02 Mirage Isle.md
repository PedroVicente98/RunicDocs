### What ArcheAge has on Mirage Isle

ArcheAge's figures, kept as a starting point. RunicGame's own numbers go into data tables. ArcheAge changed its prices between versions, so the tables show the launch prices (2014) and the later ones where they differ. The sources are listed at the end.

#### How it works

* Mirage Isle is ArcheAge's main trade hub, with vendors for nearly every major purchase. It is reached through portals placed across the world, shown on the map as green swirls.
* It sells designs (plans), not finished items: houses, farm buildings, ships, land vehicles, gliders and workbenches. The player still builds the item from the design plus materials.
* The price is in **Gilda Stars**: bound to the character, can't be sold to vendors, earned from quests (daily quests, Blue Salt Brotherhood quests, mentoring in dungeons), from trade runs across the ocean (1 to 4 stars per trade pack, by destination) and from events.
* Some house and farm designs aren't sold on Mirage Isle. They come from ArcheAge's cash shop (the Marketplace) or from quests, or are upgrades of a house already built.
* Categories on the wiki's Mirage Isle page: gliders, ships, houses (including the marine houses), farm houses, tree houses, fences and furniture, castles, and clothing.

* Tax is paid in Tax Certificates, and each house also holds a tax deposit of twice its weekly tax.
* Each faction has its own styles. Nuian cottages, manors, townhouses, villas and chalets come in Rustic, Stone and Tudor, each in Spring, Slate and Rose; villas also come as Porch, Veranda and Sunroom, and chalets as Terrace and Balcony. Haranyan houses use the Swept-roof style.
* **Not on Mirage Isle:** the Scarecrow Garden (8x8, 150 or 300 Gilda Stars), the Scarecrow Farm (16x16, 300), the Solarium and the Deserted Cottage (28x28, 1,000), and the mushroom and beanstalk houses. They come from the cash shop or from quests.
* **Upgrades:** many houses upgrade to a second tier: raised cottages; trade, apothecary and armorer villas, townhouses and manors; harvester, miner and rancher farmhouses; the storage silo (8x8); and the advanced fellowship plaza.
