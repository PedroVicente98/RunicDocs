**ID:** UNIQ-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Item progression and infinite progression#Anatomy of a weapon|Item progression and infinite progression › Anatomy of a weapon]] (unique effects and extraction)

Unique weapons are named weapons from specific bosses, PvP or rare recipes, with fixed secondary stats and a unique effect. The effect is flexible: add a tag to a skill, swap tags, change the element, add an effect or add a new skill. A weapon that adds a skill is stronger, and that is accepted.

Effects come from PvE and can also come from PvP; the ratio between the sources is a balance knob. They work in PvE and PvP.

The effect can be extracted, which destroys the source item, and sold at the auction house. Extraction is the same process as dismantling: the effect becomes a gem, which can go into another weapon's socket (one per weapon) or be crushed into another resource. The gem holds an Infusion Rune: the rune is the gem's descriptive flavor, with the same function.
