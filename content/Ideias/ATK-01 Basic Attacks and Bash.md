**ID:** ATK-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Weapon Class System#Loadouts|Weapon Class System › Loadouts]]; [[Combat#Resolve hard and light CC|Combat › Resolve: hard and light CC]] (effect gates)

Basic attacks use the left and right mouse buttons, have no cooldown and have a fixed damage type per weapon. The Quarterstaff is hybrid, physical and magical. The Bow is the only physical ranged weapon.

Discipline passives can imbue basic attacks with an element: fire builds Burn, ice builds Chill and lightning builds Shock ([[BUILD-01 Conditions, Build-up, Resolve and Tolerance|BUILD-01]]). Imbuing isn't exclusive to Arcane.

The Staff and the Instrument are magic weapons with a melee bash that stuns and pushes away. The Wand has no bash: with a Shield it uses the shield bash, and with a Focus it gets more evasion.

The bash has no cooldown, only cost and recovery, but its stun has its own effect cooldown. The effect cooldown is spent when the skill is used, hit or miss, and the effect's cost is paid only on a hit. Any skill can gate its effect the same way: by the skill's cooldown, an effect cooldown, an effect cost, or both. *(proposal: holding the button uses the version with the effect, with a heavier animation, and a tap is the plain bash, so a filler bash never spends the stun by accident.)*
