**ID:** SUB-01 · **Status:** Partial · **Game vision:** [[Business model, launch, visuals and audio#Subscription|Business model, launch, visuals and audio › Subscription]]

In Archeage, Patreon was the subscription model, i think i would do something similar.

Land Ownsership only for Subscription Users, and Labor/Energy restore offline only to Patreon Subscription. Some other minor benefits might be good, but those are the 2 main ones, the max labor/energy should be equal, unlike archeage that was 5k to 10k from free to patreon. This should be the main source of income from the RPG, so it needs to have a significant advantage but not in a annoying way for the free users, its a clear barrier that dont poke the free user constantly with surprise limitations.

**Not in [[RunicGame — Complete MMORPG Vision|the game vision]] yet:** other minor benefits.
