**ID:** HOUSE-02 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Economy, housing and land]] › Basements

* The basement is an extra built after the house.
* **The entrance comes first:** a trapdoor or a staircase.
* **Growth:** one cell per expansion, horizontally up to the plot's area or vertically. The vertical limit is still open.
* **Cost:** each expansion needs a new Market Isle design and resource packs.
* **Use:** a room like any other, and a cellar by default, for wine and aged delicacies.
* **Housing zones:** they are built with the basement enabled, and the basement dictates the ground (Architecture: [[World authoring and cooking (Engine, Game)#Housing zones and basements|World authoring › Housing zones and basements]]).

**Proposal kept here (not in the Vision):** a new level needs stairs or a trapdoor from the level above.
