**ID:** EQUIP-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Inventory, equipment and the Market Isle#Equipment slots|Inventory, equipment and the Market Isle › Equipment slots]]; [[Defense Evasion, Guard and armor#Armor|Defense: Evasion, Guard and armor › Armor]]

* **Combat (13):** main hand \+ off hand, or one 2H weapon; armor (head, chest, hands, legs, feet, with no waist or wrist); jewelry (necklace, 2 rings, 2 earrings); cloak.  
* **Back:** glider or wing. A trade pack takes it over while carried. *(proposal: the pack overrides the slot without unequipping the glider, which comes back when the pack is dropped, and the cloak is hidden while something is on the back.)*  
* **Profession (5):** head, hands, feet, body and tool, always active. *(proposal: diving gear goes here, like climbing gear; profession gear shows only during the activity.)*

There is no ranged slot: the Bow is a weapon you choose, and melee weapons have cheap ranged skills (flying slash, air ball, air pierce) at the root of their tree. There is no instrument slot ([[ART-01 Instruments, Performance and Artistry|ART-01]]) and no tool belt. The boarding hook is a single-use item used from the inventory. *(proposal: no costume or undergarment stat slots, since the account wardrobe covers looks.)*

**Armor per piece.** The iframe duration blends by the armor type of each piece, each piece counting 1/5. Guard is the sum of every piece's Guard stat, which grows with quality; cloth gives little but still adds. Total armor reduces damage through a curve that never reaches 100%. *(proposal: reduction \= A ÷ (A \+ K); with K \= 1,000, 500 armor gives 33%, 1,000 gives 50%, 2,000 gives 67% and 4,000 gives 80%.)*

**Open:** the belt lantern ([[LIGHT-01 Night and Lighting|LIGHT-01]]) lost its belt. *(proposal: an inventory item you toggle on, which burns oil.)*
