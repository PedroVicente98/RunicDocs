**ID:** ITEM-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Item progression and infinite progression]]

Make 10-20% stat difference from best in slot(balancing).

At Max Level get a good gear that is the base for this comparison.

Each expansion gives new regions new best in slot and new base good gear.

Can also upgrade previous gear to be better than the good gear that is easier to obtain but worse than best in slot, i would do a a percentage upgrade so it remains consistent between updates. Since combat is mostly skill based and should be a numbers check, gear should give bonuses lateral to just numbers updates, and may add skills and effects to skills, so using sets from different dungeons that are not in the main path.

This way any gear could potentially be best in slot, so we have item level, and best in slot is item level X, any gear could have a item level upgrade, having a max item level per expansion, and then have a since to upgrade the grade from common to mythic(but without garbage rng). Updates give small percentage boost but mostly give a cosmetic glimmer.

at max item level and mythic grade should be best in slot, skilled players could defeat a best in slot player, but there is a clear advantage.
