**ID:** DISC-02 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Weapon Class System#Skills, trees and points|Weapon Class System › Skills, trees and points]]

A Discipline can’t have skills tied to one weapon: a defense Discipline with shield skills would be useless to someone with a spear or a staff. Disciplines add skills that work with the weapon equipped. The weapon gives the form, and the Discipline gives the effect.

Discipline skills are written against what every weapon has: light and heavy attack, the off-hand action, Guard, dodge, and tags like Blunt or Finisher. Ex.: Bastion (your next Guard within 2 s reflects 30%) works as the shield block, the quarterstaff timed Guard or the spear Guard while running.

Variants by weapon (ex.: the shield adds knockback) exist only where the form really changes, using the existing interaction rules. By default, Discipline skills reuse the weapon’s animations, to keep the animation count low. Engine: a skill can bind to a weapon action (weapon.guard, weapon.heavy), plus one new condition checked at activation, “equipped weapon has tag X”.

*Superseded on 2026-10-23 by [[DISC-03 Disciplines as Skill Pools|DISC-03]]: a Discipline can also have skills exclusive to a weapon type. The rest of this entry still applies to the skills every weapon can use.*
