**ID:** DISC-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Weapon Class System#Disciplines|Weapon Class System › Disciplines]]

Each Discipline exists because it creates a different play pattern, not just a different element. Each has a skill tree and its own mechanic. Five at launch ([[DISC-04 Five Disciplines|DISC-04]]):

War (Guerra: Fury from hitting and blocking; Bleed), Defense (Vigília: shields, Guard and protecting allies), Vitality (Restauração: heals, buffs and cleanse), Arcane (Arcano: rune magic, raw arcane damage and the elemental spells) and Occultism (Ocultismo: skills paid with life, curses, stealth and teleports).

For expansions: Caça and others. Ranged damage already comes from the weapons, so it isn't a Discipline.

*Updated 2026-10-30: five Disciplines at launch (was: probably six: Restauração with Radiance, Elementalismo, Ocultismo, Canção, Guerra and Sombra; Arcano, Vigília and Caça for expansions).*
