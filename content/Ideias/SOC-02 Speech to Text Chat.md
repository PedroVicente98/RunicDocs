**ID:** SOC-02 · **Status:** Open · **Related:** [[SOC-01 Emotes and Marriage|SOC-01]]

Speech to text: the player talks and it becomes text in the chat, for the party and local chat, maybe not in global chat. It is only there to make social moments easier.

**Open:**

* Where the speech is recognized: on the player's computer (nothing but text leaves it, and the server pays nothing) or by a cloud service (billed per minute of audio).
* Whether a line is sent at once or shown first so the player can fix it before sending.
* Push-to-talk key, or speaking while a chat box is open.
* Which channels: party and local for sure; guild, whispers and global still to decide.
* Whether spoken lines are marked in the chat, and whether they follow the same limits and filters as typed ones.
