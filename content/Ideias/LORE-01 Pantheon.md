**ID:** LORE-01 · **Status:** Open

A primordial god, the origin of everything. A family pantheon of great gods with broad concepts, like Life, Time, Space and Chaos. Lesser gods descend from them, with narrower concepts, like War, Sea, Fire and Forge. Gods were always gods; none of them was ever mortal.

Chaos is one of the greatest and opens the rifts ([[RIFT-01 Chaos Rifts|RIFT-01]]), an idea inspired by Reincarnated as a Sword. Divine items are items infused with a god's power ([[GOD-01 Divine Items|GOD-01]]). *(proposal: the primordial god wrote the world in the Runic Languages.)*
