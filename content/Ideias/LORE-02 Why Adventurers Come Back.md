**ID:** LORE-02 · **Status:** Open

Players die and come back, and NPCs also come back in a different way. The story needs a reason. Options:

1. **The Anchored (preferred).** Every living being has runes that describe its life force and soul. Adventurers are rare Anchored, whose soul rune is bound to the Altars, relics of the gods of Life and Time. On death, the Altar rewrites the body from the rune, like a rewind; lost durability is the rewind's imperfection. The anchor passes through the Lineage's blood, so the Lineage remembers every Altar its characters found ([[FAM-02 Lineage and Family|FAM-02]]), but each character's own rune must be bound to an Altar before it can come back or travel there ([[ALTAR-01 Altars, Death and Respawn|ALTAR-01]]). Common NPCs die for good, but a capital's Great Altar anchors elite guards and leaders, so they come back after a while ([[CAPRAID-01 Capital Invasion|CAPRAID-01]]). Story hooks: someone who breaks anchors, and Chaos corrupting Altars.  
2. **A time loop.** The god of time and space keeps the world in a loop that brings things back to how they were. It makes permanent story deaths harder to explain.  
3. **Ignore it, like WoW.** Resurrection is gameplay flavor, and deaths in the story stay permanent. Other references: in Elder Scrolls Online the players are the Soulless; in Destiny, Ghosts revive the Guardians with the Light; in New World, the island's curse keeps people from dying.
