**ID:** CLASS-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Weapon Class System]]

Classes are named combinations of a weapon loadout (one of ten fixed loadouts, [[CLASS-02 Weapons and Named Classes|CLASS-02]]) and a Discipline, and each combination has a single role (Damage, Tank or Support). Weapon skills are the basic kit, unlocked by level; skill points go only into the Discipline skills, and each named class has one merged tree ([[DISC-03 Disciplines as Skill Pools|DISC-03]]). Skill points come only from leveling, with the same total for everyone at max level and no limit on active skills. You can pay in-game gold at an Altar to reset your whole character, redistribute the skill points and change the loadout and the Discipline.

*Updated 2026-10-16: named classes from weapons \+ Discipline (was: archeage style, a class composed of 3 different skill trees; pay money to reset).*

*Updated 2026-10-30: ten fixed loadouts, the roles Damage, Tank and Support, and points only in the Discipline (was: main hand \+ off hand or one 2H weapon; Tank, Healer or DPS; one skill tree per weapon and per Discipline; changing Vertente and Discipline).*
