**ID:** ALTAR-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Real exploration#Altars, death, respawn and teleports|Real exploration › Altars, death, respawn and teleports]]

On death, the character respawns at the nearest discovered Altar, in the same map where they died. Only Altars that character has discovered count, so finding Altars is part of exploration.

Every city has an Altar; others are scattered through the world, including islands, floating islands, the Sky Island and the Underground.

At the Altar: choosing and resetting the class (loadout and Discipline), with skill points redistributed; respawn point and teleports. Settlement Altars are teleport destinations. The Lineage sees on the map every Altar any of its characters has found, but respawn and teleports use only the Altars the character has visited, so each character has to rediscover them. A teleport costs a fixed fee plus distance, much more between continents, with no cargo fee; from the field it also uses a consumable and has a long cooldown, and from one Altar to another it costs only the fee ([[RunicGame — Appendix Nodes, Economy and Exploration|RunicGame — Appendix: Nodes, Economy and Exploration]]).

Light death penalty: equipment loses durability; maps and equipment are not lost; trade packs drop on the spot.

*Updated 2026-10-30: respawn and teleports per character, with the Lineage only seeing the Altars on the map (was: full reset of Vertente and Discipline).*
