**ID:** LAND-02 · **Status:** Open

Housing is in the open world, like ArcheAge's, with no instanced housing. The house limit is per account, there are many housing areas, and a house is kept when its tax expires ([[LAND-01 Land Ownership|LAND-01]]). Fixed plot sizes on the grid fill each housing area completely. Black Desert's shared buildings, which many players buy at once, and WildStar's private instanced island are both rejected.

**Open:** how to add housing areas over time, and how players find free plots, since searching blindly is a problem.

*(proposal: a land registry in each node's town shows the free plots on a map, free for everyone; when a node's housing areas pass a set occupancy, ex.: 85%, admins open a new area, announced in advance.)*

*Updated 2026-11-05: Housing zones are cooked with the basement tray ([[HOUSE-02 Basements and Cellars|HOUSE-02]]).*
