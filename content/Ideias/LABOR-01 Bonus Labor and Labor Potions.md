**ID:** LABOR-01 · **Status:** Open · **Related:** [[PROF-02 Professions and Masteries|PROF-02]], [[SUB-01 Subscription|SUB-01]], [[ECO-01 Economy|ECO-01]], [[Economy, housing and land]]

Quests and activities that give labor back, as bonus labor. There is one universal bonus labor bar, and it is the same bar as the labor reserve that fills when you stay away for a long time. You spend the bonus first and your normal labor after it: refill the bonus, spend the bonus, and the normal labor comes back with time. That keeps it balanced.

Labor comes back in only three ways:

* **Time:** the natural regeneration, which works for everyone. It is the only thing that refills the main labor bar.
* **The away bonus:** staying away a long time fills the bonus bar (the labor reserve).
* **Labor potions:** the only item that gives labor. Everything that returns labor outside the time system goes into the bonus bar, never into the main bar, so labor can't be stacked up endlessly.

**Labor potions** come in 3 tiers, small, medium and large, and each tier comes from a mission or activity of a different level. The large one needs a real adventure: a long quest, a raid, or a quest with a raid in it. Each tier has its own cooldown: after you use a potion of a tier, you can't use that tier again for 24 hours. The quests and activities that give potions also have a cooldown (e.g. 2, 3, 5 days or a week), set by balance, depending on how much crafted items flood the market.

Why: nobody can just keep crafting and flood the market with crafted items, because labor runs out. But the game never forces anyone to stop playing: there is always content to play. You spent all your labor crafting? Go on an epic journey, have fun playing, earn a potion and keep crafting.

*(proposal: the bonus bar keeps the labor reserve's cap, 1× the maximum labor, so potions never push it further; potions are bound and can't be traded or sold, so gold never buys labor; potions don't expire and the cooldowns are timers, not daily resets, so missing a day loses nothing ([[Overview|pillar 8]]).)*

**Open:** whether players without a subscription can use labor potions, since the labor reserve and offline regeneration are subscriber-only ([[SUB-01 Subscription|SUB-01]]); how much each tier gives; the cooldown of each source quest or activity.
