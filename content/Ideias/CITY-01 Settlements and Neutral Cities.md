**ID:** CITY-01 · **Status:** Open

Capitals, many mid-size cities and villages, spread so that each faction's civilization clearly started at one point and gets simpler the farther it goes. The neutral continent has cities at key points; the rest is exploration.

Unaffiliated neutral cities can exist as city-states or neutral fiefs, in a feudal model with a king and fiefs: a neutral fief associates with the faction that takes the region. *(proposal: the faction of the guild that owns the node.)* Faction leaders are NPCs, and players can hold faction offices, such as judge ([[JUST-01 Justice System|JUST-01]]).
