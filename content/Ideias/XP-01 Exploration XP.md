**ID:** XP-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Real exploration#Exploration XP|Real exploration › Exploration XP]]

Exploring gives its own exploration XP, separate from the level. It is spent in an exploration shop on adventurer abilities (ex.: moving objects, traversal) or on resources, and it never buys combat skills or cosmetics. The advantage this gives veterans is acceptable.
