**ID:** MOV-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Movement and vehicles]]

Exploration is a big focus: on foot, mounted, with a glider, with a wing or with the various vehicles. There should be many different kinds of vehicles. Flight exists only on the neutral continent and the Sky Island; in the rest of the world you can only glide, and over the ocean only with the wind current (see [[FLY-01 Flight Zones and Ocean Wind|FLY-01]] and [[MOUNT-01 Gliders, Wings and Mounts|MOUNT-01]]).

*Updated 2026-10-16: flight in the neutral continent and the Sky Island (was: flying can never be permanent, only gliding).*
