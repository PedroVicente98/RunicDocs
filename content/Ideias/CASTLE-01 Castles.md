**ID:** CASTLE-01 · **Status:** In game vision · **Game vision:** [[Open world]] (castles); [[Economy, housing and land]]

Castles exist only on the neutral continent: the guild that dominates a region there builds the castle in one of the region's designated zones (ex.: 3). Only one per region, fixed once placed. On the faction continents the ruling guild uses the city and an exclusive guild building. A region is a city node and its child nodes, and a guild holds at most 3 nodes or one region, never both. The mobile island with a castle is archived (see [[Archived and Discarded ideas|ARCH-01]]).

*Updated 2026-10-30: castles only on the neutral continent (was: a castle in every region).*
